# Book, cancel, or move a lesson

## Purpose

Choose a lesson time and manage changes to booked lessons.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in.
- You need an active, unexpired package with an available class for the lesson length.

## Steps

### Book a lesson

1. Open **Book** or **My classes**.
2. Choose an available day and time.
3. Confirm the lesson.
4. Check the confirmation screen and your upcoming classes.

### Cancel a lesson

1. Open the lesson in **My classes**.
2. Choose **Cancel** and confirm.
3. Read the result shown on screen.

### Move a lesson

1. Open the lesson and choose **Reschedule**.
2. Choose a new available time.
3. Confirm the change.

[Screenshot: Student booking calendar]

## Tips

- Check the date, time, and timezone before confirming.
- Your teacher’s calendar already accounts for their working hours and time off.
- Use the next-available option when you need the earliest open time.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot book.** Your package may be used up or expired, the slot may no longer be available, or the slot may be too soon under your teacher’s booking rules.

**I cannot reschedule.** Changes depend on the lesson timing and the remaining change allowance for your package. Choose Cancel if the app offers that option, or contact your teacher.

## Questions

**What happens if I cancel less than 24 hours before a lesson?** The app tells you the result before it completes the cancellation. Your teacher’s class-change rules apply.

**Why did an available slot disappear?** Another booking, a calendar change, or an updated availability rule may have made it unavailable.
