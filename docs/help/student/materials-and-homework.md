# Materials and homework

## Purpose

Find learning materials for your level and complete homework assigned to a class.

## Before you start

- Sign in to your student account.
- Homework is written and submitted in your browser, on the assignment page.

## Steps

### View materials

1. Open **Materials** or a class detail page.
2. Choose a material to read, open, download, or listen to when available.
3. Open class content from the relevant lesson whenever you need it.

You may see materials selected for your level, materials assigned directly to you, and materials attached to a class.

### Submit homework

1. Open the class with the homework card.
2. Open the assignment.
3. Write your answer. It saves as a draft while you work.
4. Add files if needed. Each file can be up to 25 MB.
5. Select **Submit**.
6. Return to the assignment later to read feedback.

[Screenshot: Student homework assignment screen]

## Tips

- Your draft is saved automatically, including when your connection drops.
- Submit before the due date when possible.
- Only submit when you are ready: the work becomes read-only unless your teacher reopens it.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot submit.** The due date may have passed without late submissions allowed, or the assignment may already be final.

**I cannot see homework feedback.** Your teacher may not have reviewed the submission yet.

## Questions

**Can I submit again?** Yes, when your teacher requests a resubmission or rejects the previous attempt.

**Can I view class content after the lesson?** Yes. Saved class content remains available from the class page.
