# View your progress

## Purpose

See the progress your teacher has chosen to share and review vocabulary when it is available.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in as a student.
- Your teacher must turn on progress sharing for you.

## Steps

1. Open **Your progress** for the relevant teacher.
2. Review the areas marked as improving and the areas worth practising.
3. If vocabulary cards are available, open one card at a time.
4. Flip the card to see its context, then choose **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy** to continue.

[Screenshot: Student progress view and vocabulary card]

## Tips

- Progress is shown separately for each teacher.
- Use the vocabulary review regularly. Choose the answer that best reflects how well you remembered it.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot see Your progress.** Your teacher may not have turned sharing on yet, or there may not be progress ready to show.

**I do not see vocabulary cards.** Vocabulary review appears only when relevant vocabulary is available.

## Questions

**Can I see my teacher’s detailed lesson notes?** No. Your progress view is a student-friendly summary.

**Can I change the progress findings?** No. Ask your teacher if you have a question about them.
