# Messages and notifications

## Purpose

Message your teacher and control how SpiralClass notifies you about lessons and learning.

## Before you start

- Sign in to your student account.
- Your teacher must be an active connection in SpiralClass.

## Steps

### Send a message

1. Open **Messages**.
2. Open your conversation with the teacher.
3. Write a message or attach a voice note, video, image, or file.
4. Send.

You can edit a text-only message within 15 minutes. You can delete a message for everyone within about two and a half days.

### Change notification settings

1. Open **Account → Notifications**.
2. Turn categories or email/push delivery on or off.
3. Save your changes.

## Tips

- Open a conversation to mark messages as read.
- If push notifications are unavailable, you may receive an email instead.
- Payment, sign-in, and certain important lesson notices cannot be turned off.

## Troubleshooting

**I am not receiving notifications.** Check the notification switches in SpiralClass and your phone’s permission for notifications.

**I cannot edit a message.** Editing is only for text-only messages during the 15-minute editing period.

## Questions

**Can I undo a deleted message?** No. Deleting a message cannot be undone.

**Why did I get an email as well as a notification?** Important notices may use every available contact method. Unread messages can also be followed by email.
