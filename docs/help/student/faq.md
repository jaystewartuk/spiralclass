# Student frequently asked questions

## Purpose

Find quick answers to common questions about using SpiralClass as a student.

## Before you start

- Sign in with the email address connected to your teacher or purchase.
- Open the linked guide when an answer needs more detail.

## Questions

### Sign-in and account

**Do I need a password?** No. Sign in with an email code or, where available, Google.

**Why is my invitation not working?** Sign in with the exact email address your teacher invited.

### Packages and booking

**Why can’t I book a lesson?** You may need an active package, an open slot, or a lesson time that meets your teacher’s booking notice.

**What happens when a package expires?** Any unused classes in that package can no longer be booked.

**Can I cancel or move a lesson?** Yes, from the class page when the lesson and your package’s remaining change allowance permit it.

### Learning

**Where are my learning materials?** Open **Materials** or the relevant class page.

**Can I submit homework in a browser?** Yes. Open the class, open the assignment, write your answer and select **Submit**.

**Why can’t I see progress reports?** Your teacher controls whether their progress view is shared with you.

### Live lessons and messages

**What should I do if I cannot join a call?** Check your connection and device permissions, then refresh the class page.

**Can I edit or remove a message?** Text-only messages can be edited within 15 minutes; messages can be deleted for everyone within about two and a half days.

## Still need help?

Check the relevant guide in the [Student Help Centre](index.md), then contact your teacher or usual SpiralClass support channel if the issue continues.

## Tips

- Check the lesson date, time, and timezone before confirming a booking.
- Keep the email on your account current so you receive sign-in codes.

## Troubleshooting

If something on screen differs from these answers, do not repeat a payment or booking. Take a screenshot and contact your teacher or support.
