# Sign in and get started

## Purpose

Access your lessons, materials, packages, and messages.

## Before you start

- Use the email address your teacher invited or the email used when you bought a package.
- Have access to that email inbox.

## Steps

1. Open **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter the six-digit code sent to your email.
4. Open **My classes** to see your lessons, or use the student tabs.

You can also choose **Sign in with Google** where it is offered.

[Screenshot: Student sign-in screen]

## Tips

- Codes expire after five minutes, so enter them promptly.
- If your teacher invited you, sign in with the same email used for the invitation.
- After buying a package from a teacher’s booking page, use the email from checkout to sign in later.

Suggested video: **“Your First Steps as a Student (1 minute)”**

## Troubleshooting

**I did not receive a code.** Check spam or junk mail, then request a new code.

**My invitation does not connect to my lessons.** Make sure you signed in with the exact invited email address.

## Questions

**Do I need a password?** No. SpiralClass signs you in with an email code or, where available, Google.
