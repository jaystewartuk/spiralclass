# Join a live lesson

## Purpose

Join your scheduled lesson and use the camera, microphone, and other call controls.

## Before you start

- Open an upcoming lesson close to its start time.
- Allow camera and microphone access when asked.
- Use a reliable internet connection and headphones when possible.

## Steps

1. Open the upcoming class in **My classes**.
2. Select **Join call** when it appears.
3. Check your camera and microphone.
4. Use the controls to mute, turn your camera on or off, share your screen, or nudge your teacher if needed.
5. Minimize the call if you need to open another part of SpiralClass; it stays active.
6. Leave the call when the lesson ends.

[Screenshot: Student live lesson call]

## Tips

- Only one person can share a screen at a time.
- On mobile, screen sharing is available on Android. It is not available on iPhone or iPad.
- Your teacher may make class materials available from the call or class page.

Suggested video: **“Join Your First Live Lesson (1 minute)”**

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot join the call.** Check your connection, refresh the class page, and allow camera and microphone permissions.

**I cannot hear anyone.** Check your device volume, headphones, and mute control.

## Questions

**Can I leave the call open while I check a message?** Yes. Minimize the call instead of leaving it.
