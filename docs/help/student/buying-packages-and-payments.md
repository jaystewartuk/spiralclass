# Buy packages and pay

## Purpose

Buy lesson packages from your teacher and understand when they are ready to use.

## Before you start

- Open your teacher’s booking page or sign in to buy again.
- Choose the package that matches the lesson length you want.

## Steps

### Pay by card

1. Choose a package.
2. Add a valid discount code if you have one.
3. Select card payment and complete the payment screen.
4. Wait for the confirmation. Your package becomes ready once payment is confirmed.

### Pay by bank transfer

Your teacher may offer a transfer instead of, or alongside, card payment —
Wise, or a bank account in her own country (SPEI in Mexico, PIX in Brazil,
and so on).

1. Choose the transfer option shown at checkout.
2. Wise gives you a payment link. A bank account gives you the account
   details to enter in your own banking app — check the bank name shown
   matches the one your app displays before you send.
3. Use the payment reference exactly as shown. It is how your teacher
   identifies your payment.
4. Send the transfer.
5. Select **I already sent it** if shown.
6. Wait for your teacher to confirm receipt. The package then becomes ready.

## Tips

- Keep the payment reference exactly as shown for a transfer. It is the only
  thing tying your payment to your package.
- A discount must be valid before checkout to reduce the price.
- Check the package expiry date. Unused classes cannot be booked after it expires.

## Troubleshooting

**My package is not active yet.** Card payments need confirmation. Transfers need your teacher to confirm receipt, which can take longer.

**My discount code does not work.** It may be expired, used up, unavailable for this package, or not valid for your account.

## Questions

**Can I buy without an account?** Yes. You can buy from a teacher’s public booking page, then sign in later using the email from checkout.

**What happens after a refund?** The payment is refunded and any remaining classes from that package are removed. We email you when it is issued, and the money goes back to the same payment method you paid with — usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.
