# Manage your account

## Purpose

Update your details, change your sign-in email, control notifications, request your data, or request account deletion.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in.
- Keep access to your email if you plan to change your sign-in address.

## Steps

### Update your details

1. Open **Account** or **Settings**.
2. Edit your name, phone number, timezone, or available profile details.
3. Save.

### Change your sign-in email

1. Open the email-change option.
2. Enter your new email address.
3. Enter the verification code sent to that new address.
4. Confirm the change.

### Request data or account deletion

1. Open the data or account section of settings.
2. Choose **Export my data** to download your information.
3. Choose **Delete account** and confirm if you want to request deletion.
4. Return to this screen to cancel a pending deletion request during the grace period.

[Screenshot: Student account settings]

## Tips

- Update your timezone before booking lessons while travelling.
- Keep your email current so you can receive sign-in codes and lesson notices.
- Account deletion has a 30-day grace period.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot change my email.** Check that you entered the code sent to the new address before it expired.

**I cannot request deletion.** Deletion is blocked while you have unused paid classes. Book, use, or resolve them first.

## Questions

**Will deleting my account happen immediately?** No. You have 30 days to cancel the request.

**Can I change notification preferences?** Yes. Open **Notifications** in account settings.
