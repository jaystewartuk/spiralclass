# Create packages and manage payments

## Purpose

Create lesson offers, record an existing package, and manage payments and refunds.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in as a teacher.
- Have the number of classes, lesson duration, price, and expiry period ready.

## Steps

### Create or edit an offer

1. Open **Settings → Templates**.
2. Add or edit an offer with a name, class count or single-class option, duration, price, and expiry period.
3. Save all changes.

Removing an existing offer archives it. It does not remove packages students already bought.

### Record a package you sold elsewhere

1. Open the student profile.
2. Choose **Add package**.
3. Enter total classes and classes remaining. Add price or expiry details if needed.
4. Save.

### Confirm a transfer or issue a card refund

1. Open the payment or package details.
2. For a bank transfer or Wise, confirm receipt only after the money has
   actually arrived in your account.
3. For an eligible card payment, choose **Refund**, add the reason, and confirm.

## What it costs you to get paid

Two different costs, from two different companies. Only the first is ours.

- **Your SpiralClass plan** — what you pay us for the software. That is the
  only money SpiralClass takes from you. We charge no commission on your lesson
  income, on any plan, including Free.
- **Stripe's processing fee** — what Stripe charges you for handling a payment
  made through Stripe. Your student pays you directly: the money goes into your
  own Stripe account, and Stripe takes its fee there before paying out to your
  bank. SpiralClass never holds that money and receives no part of that fee.
  Stripe sets the rate for your country and the method your student used, so
  check [Stripe's own pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing) for what applies to
  you. This covers **every** method Stripe offers at checkout — cards, and the
  local bank-transfer or cash options it may show your student. A bank transfer
  made inside Stripe checkout is still a Stripe payment and still carries the
  fee.
- **Wise** — your student transfers the money straight to your Wise account. It
  never passes through Stripe, so there is no Stripe fee at all. Wise or your
  own bank may still charge you for receiving money.

This is why a package can carry a lower Wise price than its Stripe price: you
keep more of a Wise transfer, so you can pass some of that on. Both prices are
yours to set, and neither is required.

Refunds and disputes are handled on your Stripe account too. Stripe's fee
policy on a refunded payment is Stripe's, not ours.

## Tips

- Set a clear expiry period. Unused classes cannot be booked after the package expires.
- Students see one combined available balance when more than one eligible package applies.
- A paid card package activates once the payment is confirmed. A transfer package activates when you confirm receipt.

## Troubleshooting

**A student cannot book.** Check that they have an active, unexpired package with a matching lesson duration.

**Why can’t I add another offer?** Free plans have a limit on active templates. Upgrade or archive an offer you no longer use.

## Questions

**Can I change a package after a student buys it?** Existing purchased packages retain their own details. Update the template for future purchases.

**What happens after a refund?** The student’s remaining credit from that payment is removed and both of you are notified.
