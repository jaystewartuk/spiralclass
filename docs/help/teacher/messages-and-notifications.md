# Messages and notifications

## Purpose

Talk with your students and choose how SpiralClass keeps you informed.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in as a teacher.
- You can message eligible students in your roster.

## Steps

### Send a message

1. Open **Messages**.
2. Open an existing conversation or select an eligible student.
3. Write a message or attach a voice note, video, image, or file.
4. Send.

To edit a text-only message, choose Edit within 15 minutes. To delete a message for everyone, choose Delete within about two and a half days.

### Change notifications

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn categories or email/push delivery on or off.
3. Save your changes.
4. To silence one student’s general notifications, open that student’s profile and adjust their notification setting.

[Screenshot: Notification preferences]

## Tips

- Open a conversation to mark its messages as read.
- Important payment, billing, and sign-in messages cannot be switched off.
- If push is unavailable, SpiralClass may send an email instead.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot edit a message.** Only text-only messages can be edited, and only during the 15-minute editing period.

**I am not getting notifications.** Check your notification settings and your phone’s permission for SpiralClass notifications.

## Questions

**Can I undo a deletion?** No. Deleted messages cannot be restored.

**Will the student know I read a message?** Opening the conversation updates the read state.
