# Student progress reports

## Purpose

Review learning insights from lessons, prepare for the next class, and choose whether a student can see a simplified progress view.

## Before you start

- Open a student profile or a completed class.
- Progress insights depend on the relevant learning features being available and on the student’s consent where requested.

## Steps

1. Open a student profile to view their focus areas and vocabulary progress.
2. Use the consent controls before enabling eligible learning insights. For a minor, complete the additional consent step.
3. Choose whether to **Share progress with student**.
4. Before a class, open the preparation card and add helpful cues to your lesson notes.
5. After an analyzed class, review each finding. Choose **Confirm**, **Edit**, or **Dismiss**, or add your own finding.

[Screenshot: Student progress and lesson preparation card]

## Tips

- Student-facing progress is simplified; students do not see your detailed evidence or internal lesson findings.
- Treat suggestions as teaching support. Review them before relying on them in a lesson.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot see insights.** The class may not yet be ready, the feature may not be available, or the required consent may be missing.

**The student cannot see progress.** Turn on **Share progress with student** from their profile.

## Questions

**Can I change a finding?** You can edit or dismiss a suggested finding before confirming it.

**What does the student see?** They see encouraging progress groupings and, where available, vocabulary review cards.
