# Manage settings and your plan

## Purpose

Update your profile and calendar connection, control notifications, and manage your SpiralClass Free or Pro plan.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in as a teacher.
- Keep access to your email if you plan to change your sign-in address.

## Steps

### Update account settings

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Edit your name, phone number, timezone, or other available profile details.
3. Save.

To change your email, enter the new address and complete the verification code sent there.

### Connect or disconnect Google Calendar

1. Open **Settings → Calendar**.
2. Choose **Connect Google Calendar** and approve access.
3. Return to SpiralClass and check that the connection is active.
4. Toggle sync off or disconnect whenever needed.

### Upgrade or manage Pro

1. Open the billing or upgrade screen.
2. Choose the available plan and complete payment.
3. Use the customer portal to update your payment method or cancel.

## Tips

- Your plan is what you pay SpiralClass for the software. It is not what it
  costs you to get paid: a payment made through Stripe carries Stripe's own
  processing fee, charged to your own Stripe account. See
  [Create packages and manage payments](packages-and-payments.md).
- Your Pro access continues until the end of a paid period if you cancel.
- After a trial ends, your account moves to Free rather than being locked.
- A failed renewal gives you seven days to update payment details before access moves to Free.

## Troubleshooting

**Why are some tools locked?** They may require Pro or be limited by your current plan.

**Why can’t I delete my account?** Account deletion is blocked while you have unused paid classes outstanding.

## Questions

**Can I export my data?** Yes. Request an export from account settings.

**Can I cancel an account deletion request?** Yes, during the 30-day grace period.
