# Join live calls

## Purpose

Join a scheduled lesson, control your camera and microphone, share your screen, and use supported teaching tools during a call.

## Before you start

- Open an upcoming scheduled class near its start time.
- Allow camera and microphone access when your device asks.
- Use a reliable internet connection and headphones when possible.

## Steps

1. Open the upcoming class from your dashboard or class list.
2. Select **Join call** when it appears.
3. Check your camera and microphone.
4. Use the call controls to mute, turn your camera on or off, share your screen, or nudge the student.
5. Minimize the call if you need to view another part of SpiralClass; the call stays active.
6. Leave the call when the lesson ends.

[Screenshot: Live lesson call controls]

## Tips

- Only one person can share a screen at a time.
- On mobile, screen sharing is available on Android; it is not available on iPhone or iPad.
- Recording and live captions appear only when they are enabled for your account and class.

Suggested video: **“Join and Run a Live Lesson (2 minutes)”**

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot join.** Refresh the class page, check your connection, and allow camera and microphone permissions.

**The student has not joined.** Use the nudge control if it is available, then wait for them to join.

## Questions

**Can I record a lesson?** If recording is available to you, start and stop it from the call controls. A recording indicator is shown while it runs.

**Can I keep the call open while checking messages?** Yes. Minimize it instead of leaving.
