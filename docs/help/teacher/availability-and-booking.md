# Set availability and manage classes

## Purpose

Choose when students can book you, block time off, and manage booked classes.

## Before you start

- You must have completed teacher setup.
- Set your timezone before adding working hours.

## Steps

### Change working hours

1. Open **Settings** and then **Availability**.
2. Add, edit, or remove your weekly time ranges.
3. Adjust buffer time, minimum notice, or booking window if needed.
4. Save.

### Block time off

1. Open **Settings** and then **Blocked dates**.
2. Tap a day on the calendar. Tap a second day to block everything in between, or block the one day on its own. You can also type the dates into the **From** and **To** fields.
3. Add a reason if you want one. A student whose class the block cancels is shown it.
4. Press **Block**.

Before you press it, the screen tells you how many classes are booked in the days you picked, and a dot under a date means a class is booked that day. If a block overlaps scheduled classes, those classes are cancelled, the class credit returns to the student, and the student is notified.

Removing a block later gives those days back and lets students book them again. It does not un-cancel the classes the block already cancelled.

### Book or cancel for a student

1. Open the student or class calendar.
2. Select an available time to book on the student’s behalf, or open a class and choose **Cancel**.
3. Give a reason when cancelling and confirm.

## Tips

- Blocked days are struck through on the calendar, so you can see what is already off before you pick.
- Students see only times that fit your working hours, blocks, buffers, and booking notice.
- You can book a student yourself even when your normal minimum notice would prevent a student from doing so.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot save my hours.** Make sure time ranges do not overlap and that at least one range remains.

**A slot disappeared.** It may have been booked, blocked, or no longer meets the booking notice rules.

## Questions

**Can I connect Google Calendar?** Yes. In **Settings → Calendar**, connect your Google account. Busy events help prevent conflicting bookings.

**Can I cancel a completed or no-show class?** Yes. Choose Cancel and provide the required reason; the class credit is returned.
