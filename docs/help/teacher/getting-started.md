# Set up your teaching account

## Purpose

Set up your profile, availability, and first class packages so students can book you.

## Before you start

- You need an email address you can open now.
- Have your teaching hours, lesson lengths, and prices ready.

## Steps

1. Choose **Create account** and enter your name and email address.
2. Enter the six-digit code sent to your email.
3. Confirm your timezone and contact details.
4. Add the days and times when you teach. Set your buffer time, minimum notice, and how far ahead students may book.
5. Create at least one package or single-class option with its duration, price, and expiry period.
6. Review your public booking page and finish setup.

[Screenshot: Teacher onboarding steps]

## Tips

- Use the timezone where you normally teach.
- Leave a buffer between lessons so students cannot book you back-to-back.
- Your booking page becomes available after setup is complete.

Suggested video: **“Set Up Your Teaching Account (3 minutes)”**

## Troubleshooting

**I did not receive a code.** Check spam or junk mail, then request a new code. Codes expire after five minutes.

**I cannot finish availability.** Add at least one working-hours range and make sure your ranges do not overlap.

## Questions

**Can I change this later?** Yes. Open Settings to edit your profile, availability, and packages.

**Do I need a paid plan to start?** No. New teachers begin with a 30-day Pro trial and no card is required.
