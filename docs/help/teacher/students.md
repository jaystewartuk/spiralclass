# Manage students

## Purpose

Add students to your roster, invite them to use SpiralClass, keep private notes, and archive a student when lessons pause.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in as a teacher.
- For an invitation, use the student’s current email address.

## Steps

### Add one student

1. Open **Students**.
2. Select **Add student**.
3. Enter the requested details and save.

### Invite a student

1. Open the student’s profile.
2. Choose **Invite** or **Resend invitation**.
3. Ask the student to open the email and sign in with that same email address.

Invitation links are valid for 30 days.

### Add many students (web)

1. Open the bulk-invite screen.
2. Paste names and email addresses, one student per line.
3. Review invalid or duplicate entries.
4. Send invitations for the valid entries.

### Add a private note or archive a student

1. Open the student profile.
2. Use **Notes** to save a private note, or choose **Archive** to pause the roster entry.
3. Add a reason if useful and confirm.

[Screenshot: Student profile with Notes and Archive actions]

## Tips

- Private notes are visible only to you.
- Archiving does not delete the student, their packages, past lessons, or their sign-in access. You can reactivate them later.
- Students added in bulk stay quiet until you choose to enable their notifications.

## Troubleshooting

**The invitation does not work.** The student must sign in with the exact email you invited. Resend the invitation if it expired.

**Why can’t I add a student?** Your plan may have reached its student limit. Check your plan or upgrade.

## Questions

**Can I change a student’s contact details?** Yes. Open their profile and save the change. A student can also update their own details.

**Does archiving cancel future lessons?** No. Review upcoming lessons separately before archiving.
