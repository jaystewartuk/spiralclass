# Teacher frequently asked questions

## Purpose

Find quick answers to common questions about using SpiralClass as a teacher.

## Before you start

- Sign in to your teacher account.
- Open the linked guide when an answer needs more detail.

## Questions

### Getting started

**How do students find me?** Share your public booking page after you finish onboarding.

**Can I use SpiralClass before paying for Pro?** Yes. New teachers start with a 30-day Pro trial, then move to Free unless they subscribe.

### Students and classes

**Does archiving remove a student?** No. It pauses that roster entry and can be reversed.

**What happens when I block dates with existing classes?** The affected classes are cancelled and the student receives their class credit back.

**Can I book a class for a student?** Yes, from your dashboard or calendar.

### Packages and payments

**When does a package expire?** On the expiry date set for that package. Remaining classes cannot be booked afterward.

**Can I record a payment received outside SpiralClass?** Yes. Add an active package from the student profile.

**Does SpiralClass take a cut of what my students pay me?** No. Your students pay you directly and we take no commission on any plan. Your plan is the only money we charge you.

**So why is a payment slightly less than the price I set?** Because Stripe charges its own processing fee for handling it, on your own Stripe account. That fee is Stripe's — SpiralClass receives no part of it — and Stripe sets the rate for your country and the method your student used. See [Stripe's pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing), or [Create packages and manage payments](packages-and-payments.md) for the full picture.

**Is there a way to avoid the Stripe fee?** Take payment through Wise instead — your student transfers straight to your Wise account, so the money never passes through Stripe and carries no Stripe fee. Your own bank or Wise may still charge you for receiving money. Note that a bank transfer offered _inside_ Stripe checkout is still a Stripe payment and still carries the fee.

### Teaching tools

**Who can see my lesson notes?** Students see only the student-facing notes during the class window. Your private cues remain private.

**Can students submit homework on the web?** Yes. They open the assignment from the class page, write their answer, attach any files and submit.

## Still need help?

Check the relevant guide in the [Teacher Help Centre](index.md), then contact your usual SpiralClass support channel if the issue continues.

## Tips

- Check your timezone, package balance, and class time before changing a booking.
- Keep student contact details current so invitations and notices arrive correctly.

## Troubleshooting

If something on screen differs from these answers, do not repeat a payment or booking. Take a screenshot and contact support.
