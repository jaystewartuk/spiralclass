# D-145. Outside Stripe, a teacher is paid only through a validated payee instrument, and Wise is the only kind

The only way to pay a teacher other than Stripe is the manual-transfer rail.
On that rail the student pays the teacher's own payee instrument directly
against a unique reference. `PayoutInstrumentKind` has a single member,
`wise`. A payment is confirmed in one of two ways: the Wise statement poller
reconciles it through her own Wise API credentials, or she confirms it by
hand. Either way it then goes through the same `advancePayment` state machine
as a Stripe payment. Instruments are written only through
`saveTeacherInstrument`, and every payee value is checked by a validator in
shared code before a student can see it.

## Why

The platform never receives a student's money ([D-143](./D-143.md)). So when
Stripe is not an option, she needs a rail where the student pays her
directly. That covers the five countries Stripe refuses as merchants (IN, ZA,
NG, ID, IS), every country outside the 44 measured ones, and any teacher whose
Stripe account is restricted. On this rail, a wrong payee value is how real
money gets lost for good. A student who sends a transfer to a mistyped account
has paid a stranger, and nothing can undo it.

## Options considered

- **Wise as a payment provider** (launch to 2026-08-23) — the rail was never
  really Wise-shaped. Code branched on `provider === "wise"` in 30 files, so a
  second instrument would have needed its own copy of the mark-sent →
  confirm → activate flow.
- **A sibling `spei` provider, or one kind per country** — duplicates code
  that then drifts apart, and every new country needs a migration.
- **`bank_account` with a 15-scheme per-country registry** (2026-08-23 to
  2026-08-31) — supported CLABE, PIX, NUBAN, IFSC, IBAN, SWIFT/BIC and others,
  each with its real checksum where the format has one. It was removed for
  two reasons:
  - Every payment needed the teacher to confirm it by hand, permanently. No
    retail bank offers a per-teacher statement API to reconcile against.
  - Once she became merchant of record, her own Stripe account offered SPEI at
    checkout and confirmed it automatically. The same buyer saw the local
    bank transfer twice on one page.
- **Keeping `bank_account` unused** — it would have stayed as about 55 files,
  a 1,000-line registry with a 491-line test, a Postgres enum, three columns,
  four CHECK constraints, payee forms on two clients and 120 i18n keys. All of
  it would need maintaining and reasoning around.
- **A free-form payee-details field** — rejected from the start. An
  unvalidated payee field on a page someone pays from loses money with no way
  back.

## What it cost

- **A teacher in IN, ZA, NG, ID or IS has exactly one rail.** If she cannot
  or will not use Wise, she cannot be paid at all.
- **Stripe is a single point of failure for a teacher without a Wise
  instrument.** She can add one, but nothing prompts her to.
- **Supporting a country's bank accounts again would take a migration**,
  where under the registry it was just a new row.
- **Nobody measured the trade.** No student was ever shown both Stripe's SPEI
  and a manual SPEI side by side, so "the duplicate cost conversion" comes from
  how the page looked, not from a funnel.
- **The reference is the only way to match a payment.** Confirmation depends
  on the teacher or the poller.
  [transfer-confirm-reminder.ts](../../apps/web/src/lib/payments/transfer-confirm-reminder.ts)
  is what stops a forgetful teacher leaving a paid package unactivated.

## What it constrains

- **No free-form payee field.** A new kind needs a validator in
  `packages/shared` and a truthful `supportsAutoReconcile()` answer before the
  settings UI can offer it. A card link held by the teacher (a Wise payment
  link, a Mercado Pago link de pago, PayPal) is allowed only on those terms.
- **`saveTeacherInstrument`
  ([save-instrument.ts](../../apps/web/src/lib/payments/save-instrument.ts))
  is the only write path.** The SQL listing gate (`HAS_PAYOUT_RAIL_WHERE`)
  and the JS readiness check agree only because nothing else writes this
  table.
- **Keep `kind` an enum** and keep `TeacherPayoutInstrument` as its own
  table. A second kind would come back through them. Guarded by "is closed to
  wise alone" in
  [payout-instruments.test.ts](../../packages/shared/src/payout-instruments.test.ts).
- **Keep `PaymentRail.bank_transfer`.** Nothing writes it now, but older rows
  carry it and must not be reinterpreted.
- **Keep the two provider enums apart.** `PaymentProvider` (`stripe |
manual_transfer`) is the student-to-teacher rail, where the platform never
  receives the money. `SubscriptionPaymentProvider` (`stripe | manual`) is
  what she pays SpiralClass, where the platform does receive it.
- **An instrument is offered only at a currency it can carry**
  (`instrumentSupportsCurrency`).
- **Analytics record the instrument kind** (`wise`), not the provider, so
  funnels stay continuous across changes to the model.
- **Old links keep working.** `/b/[slug]/buy/wise/[ref]` redirects to
  `/b/[slug]/buy/transfer/[ref]`.
- **Before a migration that drops data, check the table, not the event
  stream** (see below).

## How we got here

- **At launch:** Wise was a `PaymentProvider` value backed by seven columns on
  `teachers`.
- **2026-08-23:** the manual rail was made generic, with the instrument stored
  as data. A count showed 231 non-test files mentioning Wise, but only 30
  branching on the provider. That day:
  - `PaymentProvider` became `stripe | manual_transfer`, and subscription
    billing got its own enum.
  - `wise_price_centavos` became `transfer_price_centavos`, the non-card
    price it had always been.
  - SPEI was folded into `bank_account` behind the scheme registry the same
    day, before either change shipped.
  - The currency list grew from six currencies to about fifty.
- **2026-08-31:** `bank_account` was removed. The migration raised an
  exception instead of deleting any payment attached to such an instrument.
  - The first draft said production held zero such payments, based on PostHog
    `payment_received`. That event fires only on a **paid** payment, and
    production held two abandoned pending checkouts against an `mx_clabe`
    instrument. They were deleted on purpose before the promote.
  - Preview tripped the guard on seeded fixtures added that same morning.
  - Most all-time `payment_received` rows turned out to be seeded preview data,
    because `execute-sql` does not apply the test-account filter. Don't re-quote
    figures taken without the environment split.
- **Since then:** the legacy wire keys (`clabe`, `clabeFormatted`,
  `schemeOptions` and others), which the removal had kept as constant nulls,
  are gone from the code. The code wins over the original record here.
