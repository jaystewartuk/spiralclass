# D-64. Teacher-selectable pricing currency, split by payout rail

**What changed.** Every teacher's packages/prices were hardcoded to MXN
regardless of the teacher's actual country or market, via `currencyForTeacher()`
in `packages/shared/src/platform-regions.ts` — it always resolved to
`PLATFORM_REGIONS.MX.currency` ("MXN") because that was the only registry
entry. This decision replaces that hardcoding with a **teacher-chosen pricing
currency**, picked once from a curated list at onboarding (same
capture-once-never-changes pattern as `Teacher.country` —
`apps/web/prisma/schema.prisma:244-253`) and never self-service-editable
afterward.

`Teacher.pricingCurrency` becomes the new single source of truth for "what
currency is this teacher's class pricing denominated in" — a genuinely
different concept from `Teacher.platformRegion` ("which platform Stripe
entity settles this teacher's money", currently MXN for everyone because
there is exactly one platform entity). Conflating the two was the original
bug: a teacher's _display/charge_ currency should never have been derived
from which platform Stripe entity happens to exist today.

**Why it likely changed.** SpiralClass's growth market is teachers outside
Mexico — the Wise rail exists specifically to serve any country outside
Stripe Connect's cross-border payout circle. Charging every one of those
teachers' students in MXN regardless of the teacher's real market is a
retention/conversion tax with no technical justification: the Wise
reconciliation matcher already compares `credit.currency` to
`payment.currency` per payment, and Stripe Connect's `Transfer` already
derives currency from the real settled `balance_transaction` — nothing
downstream _requires_ MXN, it was just never made teacher-configurable.

**What was affected.**

- **Data model** — new `Teacher.pricingCurrency` column (`String`, default
  `"MXN"` to match current behavior for the two existing rows; backfill is
  trivial since 100% of existing teachers are already MXN-priced).
  Enforcement is an app-level `isPricingCurrencySupported()` validator in
  `packages/shared` (mirroring `isConnectCountrySupported()`'s shape), not a
  DB CHECK constraint — a shared-package constant can grow the curated list
  without a migration, matching the reasoning in `countries.ts` for why
  `SUPPORTED_CONNECT_COUNTRIES` also isn't a CHECK enum.
- **Currency resolution** — `currencyForTeacher()` now reads
  `teacher.pricingCurrency` (falling back to `"MXN"` only for the
  shouldn't-happen case of a null value, never throwing).
- **Onboarding + settings UI** — a currency picker gated by
  `isConnectCountrySupported(teacher.country)`, sequenced after country is
  captured (country must be known first to pick the right list). Locked
  after onboarding.
- **Checkout + package creation** — `apps/web/src/app/actions/checkout.ts`,
  `start-checkout.ts` and `onboarding.ts`
  thread `currencyForTeacher(teacher)` through to the Stripe Checkout
  Session's `currency` param and the Package/Payment rows, replacing any
  remaining hardcoded `"MXN"`/`"mxn"` literals in those paths.
- **Wise reconciliation fetch layer** — `apps/web/src/lib/wise/api.ts`'s
  `platformCurrency()` currently reads a single platform-wide env var
  (`WISE_BALANCE_CURRENCY`, default `"MXN"`) to decide which ONE balance to
  pull statement lines from, for every teacher. This becomes per-teacher,
  read from `teacher.pricingCurrency` and threaded through
  `wiseClientForTeacher(creds)`. Without this fix, a non-MXN-priced Wise-rail
  teacher's incoming credits are never fetched for reconciliation at all —
  Parts 1-4 alone don't make Wise-rail teachers actually work.

**Curated lists (by payout rail).**

- **Connect-circle rail** (`isConnectCountrySupported(teacher.country)` true
  — GB/US/CA/CH/EEA): currency flows through Stripe (charge → platform
  balance → Transfer), so the list starts small and Stripe-native: **GBP,
  USD, EUR**. All three are natively supported by a UK-domiciled Stripe
  account (Stripe's presentment-currency list is broad, but these three are
  the account's home/most-liquid currencies — no exotic-currency risk).
- **Wise rail** (everyone else): confirmed by reading `start-checkout.ts` —
  the Wise rail **never creates a Stripe Checkout Session** (only
  `paymentMethod === "stripe"` does); the Wise flow is a Quick-Pay
  instructions page keyed off the same `currencyForTeacher(teacher)` value
  used to stamp Package/Payment rows. So Stripe's presentment-currency
  support is **irrelevant** to the Wise-rail list — it's gated purely by
  whether a currency is a real, tradeable Wise Business balance currency.
  Real-usage sizing: queried the production DB
  (`select country, count(*) from teachers group by country`) — **2
  teachers total, both `MX`**. There is no real non-MXN distribution yet to
  size a broader list from; this launch is pre-growth-market. Per the operator's
  confirmation, the Wise-rail list is seeded ahead of real signal, betting on
  CLAUDE.md's stated LatAm growth-market framing rather than waiting for
  volume: **MXN, COP, ARS, CLP, PEN, BRL**.

**Explicit rejection: do not drop Stripe Connect.** This was researched and is
not being reopened casually. Stripe Connect exists so that the platform does not
have to be authorised to move the money itself — Stripe's licences cover the
payout leg. Routing student
payments through the platform's own plain Stripe account and manually
redistributing to teachers via the platform's own Wise Business account
instead of Connect is exactly the fact pattern UK payment services
regulation requires authorization for (collecting customer money then
redistributing it to a third party), and the "commercial agent" exemption
doesn't apply because the platform would be acting on behalf of both the
student and the teacher. If this comes up again, the answer is still no
without new legal input.

**Deliberately deferred (not built here).**

- Stripe multi-currency settlement, `on_behalf_of`, or opening
  currency-native bank accounts — these reduce the double-FX cost of what
  this decision builds; they don't block it. Follow-up, gated on real
  per-currency volume data once teachers actually use non-MXN currencies.
- A self-service "change my currency" settings toggle — existing
  packages/prices/payments are already denominated in the old currency;
  letting a teacher flip currency post-onboarding would corrupt that
  history. A currency change is a support-request/manual-migration
  situation, same posture as a `country` change.
- Per-student dynamic currency conversion/display (Stripe calls this
  Adaptive Pricing) — students see the teacher's one chosen currency, not a
  per-viewer localized price.

**Unresolved risks.**

- The Wise-rail curated list has **no real usage data to size it from** (2
  teachers, both MX) — it's necessarily a bet on the growth market rather
  than a data-driven cut (confirmed with the operator before implementation). Revisit
  the list once there's real non-MX teacher signup volume.
- `SUPPORTED_CONNECT_COUNTRIES` intentionally stays narrower than
  `COUNTRY_CODES` (the full onboarding country picker) — a teacher outside
  the Connect circle but also outside the initial Wise-rail currency list
  has no working pricing currency until the list is widened. This is a
  known gap, not a bug: widen the Wise-rail list additively as real markets
  show up, same posture as `SUPPORTED_CONNECT_COUNTRIES` itself.
