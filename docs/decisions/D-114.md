# D-114. Turn transcription, class recording and material podcasts off in both environments before first promotion

> **Reversed by [D-131](./D-131.md) (2026-08-26).** Recording and transcription
> are on again in both environments, and the four privacy-policy sentences this
> record's flag flip made true were corrected back. What survives is the part
> that was never about the flags: the capture bug found below — audio was
> reaching storage before the gate that was supposed to stop it — and the
> podcast flag this record created, which is still off.

**What changed.** Three student-facing AI capabilities are switched off on
**both** preview and production, and the marketing pages stop advertising
them (2026-08-08, while working the §2.3–2.4 homepage live/not-live pass).

1. **`LESSON_INSIGHTS_TRANSCRIPTION_ENABLED` and `CLASS_RECORDING_ENABLED`
   are commented out** in `config/env/preview.runtime.env` and
   `config/env/production.runtime.env`. This reverses two of the three gated
   flags [D-94](./D-94.md) turned on in
   production on 2026-07-21. `LIVE_CAPTIONS_ENABLED` — the third —
   is **untouched and still on**: captions are ephemeral (streamed from the
   teacher's browser, translated, published into the room, never stored), so
   they do not carry the recording/retention exposure the other two do.
   Preview is turned off alongside production deliberately: a capability
   that is off in production must not be exercisable on preview, or preview
   stops rehearsing the real thing.

2. **A new `MATERIAL_PODCASTS_ENABLED` flag** (`lib/env.ts`
   `podcastsEnabled()`), unset in both environments. Podcasts were the one
   student-facing AI surface with **no enablement flag at all** — availability
   was decided purely by whether `GOOGLE_TTS_API_KEY` or
   `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` happened to be present in Infisical. So "turn
   podcasts off" had no version-controlled answer; it meant pulling a
   secret, which is an unreviewable change that leaves no diff and no
   history. The flag gives podcasts the same two-condition shape every other
   AI capability here already has (a vendor is configured **and** an explicit
   flag is on), and `hasTtsCreds()` goes back to being pure credential
   detection. Five call sites moved from `hasTtsCreds()` to
   `podcastsEnabled()`, including the server-side guard in
   `lib/materials/podcast.ts` — so this is a real refusal, not just hidden
   UI. Already-generated podcasts keep playing; nothing is deleted.

3. **`maybeStartLessonAudioCapture` now also requires
   `transcriptionEnabled()`** (`lib/video/call-recording.ts`). This is a bug
   fix the flag flip exposed rather than a policy choice — see below.

4. **The marketing pages stop advertising all three.** AI lesson insights and
   study podcasts are removed from the landing page and `/features` (joining
   pronunciation scoring, cut earlier in the same branch), their 8 catalog
   keys deleted across all 3 locales, and
   `apps/web/tests/config/ai-marketing-claims.test.ts` extended so re-adding
   any of them without turning its flag on fails CI. The vocabulary/SM-2 copy
   is **softened rather than cut**: it said "each lesson's vocabulary feeds a
   spaced-repetition schedule", and the automatic per-lesson derivation is
   exactly what went away with transcription. `VocabularyReview` rows seed
   from _confirmed_ vocabulary-category insights, which a teacher can author
   by hand (`insight-actions.ts` `addInsightFor`, `source: "teacher"`), so
   the scheduler still works — hence "vocabulary from your classes".

**Why it likely changed.** The §2.3–2.4 pass established that promoting the
product on a capability which records and transcribes a lesson — potentially
including minors — is a materially louder act than having it quietly available to
one consenting pilot pair. D-114 narrows D-94's scope for the promotion window
rather than disputing it.

**What was affected.** `config/env/{preview,production}.runtime.env`;
`lib/env.ts` (new `podcastsEnabled()`); the 5 podcast gate call sites;
`lib/video/call-recording.ts`; `apps/web/src/app/page.tsx` and
`app/features/page.tsx`; all 3 i18n catalogs; `apps/web/.env.example`;
`docs/features/lesson-insights-reports.md`; and the marketing-claims guard
plus `tests/video/call-recording.test.ts`.

**The capture bug this exposed.** `LESSON_INSIGHTS_TRANSCRIPTION_ENABLED`
did **not** mean what its own module header claims ("nothing is captured or
analyzed"). It gated `on-lesson-audio-ready.ts`, which bails before sending
audio to an ASR vendor — but by then `maybeStartLessonAudioCapture` had
already captured per-participant audio and uploaded it to R2, where it sat.
That function gates on `provider.recordingConfigured()` (egress S3
credentials) and per-pair consent, and — by deliberate design, fixing a
different bug documented in `VIDEO_ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT.md` §5.2 — **not** on
`CLASS_RECORDING_ENABLED`. So turning both flags off would have stopped
transcription and hidden the Record control while student voice kept landing
in storage. Audio that can never be transcribed has no product value and
pure liability, so the gate now sits at the start of the pipeline. The
independence from `CLASS_RECORDING_ENABLED` that §5.2's fix established is
preserved; only `transcriptionEnabled()` was added.

**Unresolved risks.**

- **Whether the TTS keys were ever actually set in production cannot be
  determined from this repo**, so it is not known whether podcasts were live
  before this or already dark for want of a key. The flag makes them off either
  way, which is why the ambiguity did not block the change.
- **The flags are non-secret config, so turning them off is a deploy, not a
  secrets sync.** Production picks this up on the next promote, not on
  merge — `main` only deploys preview. Until a promote runs, production is
  still serving D-94's settings.
- **A teacher mid-flow may notice the Record control disappear** with no
  in-product explanation. No migration, notice, or changelog entry is part
  of this change.
- This narrows D-94 rather than superseding it; D-94's LiveKit half (both
  environments on the self-hosted Oracle box) is untouched and still current.
