# D-24. Student acquisition is an in-app teacher-amplification capability, not a marketplace

**What changed.** Opened a new product area — helping a teacher fill her own
roster — and committed it to a specific shape. New tenant-scoped `Lead` and
`Testimonial` tables, a public lead-capture form and teacher-authored
testimonials on `/b/<slug>`, a `/dashboard/leads` inbox and
`/dashboard/testimonials` editor, and a typed PostHog funnel (`lead_captured`,
`lead_contacted`, `lead_converted`, `lead_archived`). Strategy:
`the student-acquisition design`. This is Phase 1 (booking-page
conversion); Phases 2 (referrals) and 3 (shareable reach) are designed, not
built.

**Why it likely changed.** The product solves operations well, but the first
teacher (Alicia Moreno) has spare capacity and not enough students — demand is the
bottleneck. The platform did nothing for that: students arrive only via a
manually shared `/b/<slug>` link, and the page was buy-or-leave. Two structural
choices were made deliberately:

- **In-app, not a separate project.** Acquisition is inseparable from teacher
  identity, the booking page, checkout, auth, and the future discount/credit
  primitive. A second app would be another frontend on the same Postgres plus a
  second deploy/CI pipeline for no isolation gain.
- **Amplify the teacher, not build a marketplace.** For one teacher, a directory
  only pays off at dozens-of-teachers / hundreds-of-students scale and reverses
  the PRD's "no marketplace" stance. The optimum near-term lever is converting
  the traffic and word-of-mouth she already drives.

**What was affected.** `apps/web/prisma/schema.prisma` + migration
`20260625600000_student_acquisition` (RLS owner policies, mirrors
`student_notes`); `src/app/actions/{leads,testimonials}.ts`;
`src/lib/leads/notify-teacher.ts`; `src/app/b/[slug]/page.tsx` + `lead-form.tsx`;
`src/app/(app)/dashboard/{leads,testimonials}/`;
`src/app/(app)/dashboard/page.tsx` (new tiles + new-lead badge);
`src/lib/analytics/posthog.ts`; `src/lib/validators.ts` (`leadCaptureSchema`).
Lead capture and testimonials are available on every tier — getting paid and
getting students are never paywalled.

**Unresolved risks.** Phase 2 (referrals) depends on a discount/credit primitive
that still doesn't exist (`the referrals design`). Leads are a
pure hand-off today (no link to a roster/onboarding-hold student). Testimonials
are teacher-authored only — student-submitted reviews need moderation and are
deferred until volume. The marketplace remains a possible much-later pivot that
would need its own decision reversing the PRD.
