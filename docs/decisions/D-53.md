# D-53. Each availability rule is frozen in the time zone it was written in, and a teacher changing her zone does not move it

`availability_rules.timezone` (IANA, `NOT NULL`) records the zone a rule's
weekday and `HH:MM` wall clock were written against, and slot generation in
[`lib/slots.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/slots.ts) interprets each rule in that
zone, not in the live `teachers.timezone`. Changing the account zone leaves
existing rules where they were and tells the teacher so; re-saving Working
hours restamps every rule to her current zone.

## Why

Rules used to store a bare weekday and local time with no zone and were read
against whatever `Teacher.timezone` was at slot-generation time. A teacher who
moved country and updated her zone silently reinterpreted every rule, while her
existing bookings — stored as UTC instants — stayed put. Her declared
availability and her real booked times drifted apart with nothing to say so.
Teachers are anywhere in the world and do relocate, so this was not
hypothetical.

## Options considered

- **Rewrite every rule's clock when the zone changes.** Lost to the snapshot,
  which keeps already-booked instants stable by construction, needs no data
  migration of `Booking` rows, and has no window in which rules and bookings
  disagree.
- **Keep reading rules in the live teacher zone.** The bug above.
- **Snapshot the zone on each rule (chosen).** Booked instants stay stable by
  construction, no `Booking` rows move, and the fix is confined to the read
  path plus the write sites that stamp the zone.

## What it cost

- A zone change is now a two-step act for the teacher: change the zone, then
  re-save Working hours. If she ignores the warning her hours stay in the old
  zone; if she re-saves, every future slot shifts by the offset. Both are
  intended, and both are surfaced: `updateMyTeacherContactAction` in
  [`app/actions/teacher-account.ts`](../../apps/web/src/app/actions/teacher-account.ts)
  returns a "your working hours are still set in `<zone>`" message, and the
  Working-hours settings page shows the same as a banner.
- A save replaces the whole rule set, so in practice all of a teacher's rules
  share one zone and the per-rule column is effectively per-teacher. The read
  path handles mixed zones; there is no UI to create them.

## What it constrains

- **Never interpret a rule in `teacher.timezone` when it has its own.**
  `teacher.timezone` is only a defensive fallback in `slots.ts` for a rule with
  an empty zone. Guarded by the "per-rule timezone binding" suite in
  [`tests/slots.test.ts`](../../apps/web/tests/slots.test.ts).
- **Every write site stamps a zone.** The availability save stamps the
  teacher's current zone
  ([`tests/onboarding/save-availability-timezone.test.ts`](../../apps/web/tests/onboarding/save-availability-timezone.test.ts)).
  A new writer of `AvailabilityRule` must do the same.
- **Never silently restamp rules a teacher has written.** The one exception is
  the starter rules: provisioning seeds them in `FALLBACK_TIMEZONE` (UTC,
  `lib/auth.ts`) before her zone is known, and the onboarding timezone step
  restamps them only while `availabilityTouchedAt` is null
  ([`app/actions/onboarding.ts`](../../apps/web/src/app/actions/onboarding.ts),
  covered by `tests/actions/onboarding-timezone-action.test.ts`). Once she has
  submitted real hours they are hers.
- Bookings stay UTC instants; nothing here changes the UTC slot math.
- Behaviour detail: [scheduling](../features/scheduling-booking.md) rule 6.

## How we got here

- **2026-07-08.** Migration `20260708010000_add_availability_rule_timezone`
  added the column, backfilled it from each teacher's zone, then set it
  `NOT NULL`. The zone-change warning and banner shipped with it. The same
  change collapsed several null-zone fallbacks for display (device-local, a
  hard-coded market zone, `Intl` resolution) into one helper. That helper is
  gone from the code: the last-resort display zone is now `FALLBACK_TIMEZONE`
  (UTC) in `packages/shared/src/dual-zone.ts`, chosen because a real market
  zone renders a plausible wrong time.
- **Later.** Signup began seeding starter rules before the teacher's zone was
  known, which left a teacher who skipped the availability step with rules
  frozen in the seed zone; the onboarding restamp guarded by
  `availabilityTouchedAt` closed that.
