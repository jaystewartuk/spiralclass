# D-53. Freeze each availability rule in the zone it was written in (per-rule timezone snapshot)

**What changed.** `AvailabilityRule` now carries a `timezone` column (IANA,
NOT NULL, backfilled from the teacher's current zone). Slot generation
(`lib/slots.ts`) interprets each rule — its weekday and `HH:MM` wall clock —
in **that frozen zone**, not the live `Teacher.timezone`. The rule means what
it meant when written. Bookings stay UTC (already correct); nothing about the
DST-safe UTC math changed, only what zone the wall clock binds to.

Because a rule can no longer be silently reinterpreted, a teacher changing
their account zone is made **explicit instead of silent**: the web
`updateMyTeacherContactAction` detects rules left
in a different zone and surface a "your working hours are still set in
`<old zone>`" notice; the Working-hours settings page shows the same warning as
a banner (re-saving restamps the rules to the new zone, shifting every slot).

Student timezone is confirmed **refreshable** on both surfaces (the account
forms already allow overwrite + suggest the device zone; the one-shot
auto-capture stays non-clobbering by design). The scattered null-timezone
fallbacks (device-local vs. hardcoded `America/Mexico_City` vs.
`Intl…resolvedOptions`) collapse into one helper, `resolveDisplayZone`: saved
zone → device zone → platform default.

**Why it likely changed.** Availability rules stored a naive weekday + local
`HH:MM` with **no zone** and were interpreted against whatever
`Teacher.timezone` happened to be at slot-generation time. A teacher who
relocated and updated their zone silently reinterpreted **every existing
rule**, while already-booked `Booking` rows (stored UTC) did not move — so the
teacher's declared availability and their real booked instants desynced,
unguarded. Global launch means teachers actually move between countries, so
this stopped being hypothetical. Option (a) — a per-rule snapshot — was chosen
over (b) rewriting the clock on every zone change because it keeps existing
booked instants stable by construction (no data migration of `Booking` rows,
no window where the two disagree) and localizes the fix to the read path.

**What was affected.**

- `apps/web/prisma/schema.prisma` + migration
  `20260708010000_add_availability_rule_timezone` (additive, backfilled, then
  `SET NOT NULL`).
- `apps/web/src/lib/slots.ts` — `SlotAvailabilityRule.timezone` (required);
  per-rule zone resolution with `teacher.timezone` as a defensive fallback.
- Write sites stamp the teacher's zone: `saveAvailabilityAction` and the signup
  seed (`starterAvailabilityFor`, used by `lib/auth.ts`).
- Wire mappers thread `timezone` through: `SlotInputsWire` and the slots /
  reschedule-slots routes (callers passing raw Prisma rows already carry it).
- Teacher zone-change warning: `teacher-account.ts` and the
  `settings/availability` banner, plus the additive `staleAvailabilityZone`
  field.
- Null-tz unification: `resolveDisplayZone` / `deviceTimezone` /
  `DEFAULT_DISPLAY_TZ`, consumed by `CalendarTimeGrid`, `SingleClassSlotPicker`
  (teacher-zone default), and the account screens.

**Unresolved risks.** All of a teacher's rules share one zone in practice (a
save replaces the whole set), so the per-rule column is effectively per-teacher
today; a future feature that mixes zones within one teacher would be handled
correctly by the read path but has no UI. Re-saving Working hours after a move
restamps rules to the new zone and shifts every future slot — intended, and
guarded by the banner, but a teacher who ignores the warning can still move
their availability by an offset. The additive `staleAvailabilityZone` field is
purely additive, so older clients ignore it.
