# D-131. Turn class recording and lesson transcription back on in both environments

**What changed.** `CLASS_RECORDING_ENABLED` and
`LESSON_INSIGHTS_TRANSCRIPTION_ENABLED` are set to `1` in
`config/env/preview.runtime.env` and `config/env/production.runtime.env`,
reversing [D-114](./D-114.md) (2026-08-08), on 2026-08-26. The
privacy policy is corrected in the same change, because four of its sentences
said in plain words that these features were switched off.

No pipeline code changed. The implementation has been complete end-to-end since
D-19's Phases A to F; what D-114 removed was two lines of non-secret config, and
what this restores is the same two lines.

**Why it changed.** The condition D-114 was holding these flags behind no longer
described the business by the time the rest of the legal surface was brought into
line on 2026-08-25 (the Terms, the retention periods, the account-deletion copy),
so the hold was keeping a built, tested capability dark for a reason that had
lapsed. The reasoning itself is not published here — see [D-159](./D-159.md).

What has NOT changed is the control that actually decides whose voice is
captured: the recorded per-pairing consent (**[D-22](./D-22.md)**,
`lib/lesson-notes/consent.ts`), which is guardian-only for a student under 18
and defaults to unset for every pairing that exists today. A flag turns the
capability on. Consent decides, per student, whether anything is captured. That
separation is why turning the flags on is not the same act as turning capture
on for anyone.

**What was affected.**

- `config/env/{preview,production}.runtime.env` — the two flags, plus rewritten
  comment blocks. Production picks this up on the next `pnpm promote`, not on
  merge.
- `packages/shared/src/legal/privacy-policy.ts` — four now-false statements,
  and the AI-features list gained the entry it was missing:
  - what we collect: "Today it is switched off" removed; replaced with the
    consent rule.
  - AI features: a new line describing class transcription and learning
    insights (Deepgram for the transcript, Anthropic for the suggestions,
    audio deleted once transcribed).
  - AI features closing text: dropped "switched off in production, so no class
    audio reaches any AI vendor today".
  - children: dropped "switched off in production entirely", keeping "off by
    default for everyone" — which is still exactly true.
  - retention: "Live class audio and video — Not stored at all" was wrong the
    moment recording came on. Replaced with two honest rows: the insights audio
    (deleted once transcribed) and a teacher-started recording plus its
    transcript (kept while the account is open, deleted sooner on request).
    The lawful-basis table already carried a **Consent** row for
    "Record, transcribe or analyse a class" — nothing to add there.
- `apps/web/tests/config/ai-marketing-claims.test.ts` — the two assertions that
  the flags are off become assertions that they are on, the insights claim keys
  move from `GATED_OFF_CLAIMS` to `CLAIM_GATES`, and a new test fails if the
  "switched off" prose returns while the flags say otherwise.
- `docs/features/lesson-insights-reports.md` — status section.

**What this does NOT turn on.** `LESSON_INSIGHTS_PRONUNCIATION_ENABLED` and
`MATERIAL_PODCASTS_ENABLED` stay off — neither was asked for, and pronunciation
scoring additionally streams student audio to a **second** vendor (Azure), which
is its own decision. The marketing cards D-114 cut are **not** restored: nothing
obliges a live capability to be advertised, and the copy can come back whenever
the operator wants it, now that the guard permits it.

**What is required beyond this commit, and is not in it.**

1. **A promote.** Merging to `main` deploys nothing (D-120). Production keeps
   serving D-114's settings until `pnpm promote` runs.
2. **`DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` in production Infisical.** Without it
   `transcriptionEnabled()` is false whatever the flag says, and the whole
   pipeline stays dormant with green CI. Live captions already need this key,
   so it is probably present — verify with
   `fly secrets list --app agendaprofe`, don't assume. `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is
   needed too, for the focus areas and the pre-class brief; it is already
   required by several shipped features.
3. **`LIVEKIT_EGRESS_S3_*` on both Fly apps.** Tofu-owned and pushed straight
   to Fly (D-65), so they appear in no file in this repo. Recording is a no-op
   without them (`recordingConfigured()`).
4. **A recorded insights consent per student.** The teacher sets it on the
   student's detail page. Nothing is captured for a pairing that has none, and
   none exists today.
5. **The teacher must be on Pro** for the visible Record control
   (`gateProFeature(teacherId, "lesson_notes")`). Every new teacher starts on a
   30-day Pro trial.

**Unresolved risks and known gaps.**

- **Preview cannot rehearse this end-to-end.** The self-hosted LiveKit box
  posts webhooks to exactly one URL — production's — in
  `infra/oracle-runner/services/livekit.yaml.tftpl`, while both environments
  share the box (D-94). On preview an egress therefore never finalises, no
  `lesson.audio.ready` fires, and nothing is transcribed. Adding preview's URL
  to `webhook.urls` and running `tofu apply` is the fix; it is deliberately not
  in this commit, because it means every production call's webhooks also hit
  the preview app (harmless — every handler is keyed on ids that preview's
  database does not have — but it is a change to shared infrastructure, and one
  the operator should make knowingly). Until then, validate on production with
  a real consenting pairing.
- **The auto-capture path races track publication.**
  `maybeStartLessonAudioCapture` fires on `participant_joined` and immediately
  asks LiveKit for each participant's microphone **track**, which may not be
  published yet at that instant. There is no `track_published` handler, so a
  speaker missed at join is not retried. The reliable trigger is the teacher
  tapping **Record** — `startBookingRecording` starts both captures at a point
  where both tracks certainly exist. Practical rule for now: insights follow
  the Record tap; the automatic path is a best-effort supplement, which is what
  its own doc comment already calls it.
- **The ASR language is hardcoded to Spanish.**
  `DEFAULT_LESSON_LANGUAGE = "es"` in `lib/transcription/config.ts`, predating
  the language-first architecture of [D-72](./D-72.md). It is correct for the
  one teacher live today, and wrong for the next teacher who does not teach
  Spanish. Not fixed here because it is a behaviour change rather than an
  enablement, but it is the first thing to fix before a second teacher's
  classes are transcribed.
- **Nothing purges recordings from R2 when an account closes.** Account
  deletion anonymises columns (`lib/account-deletion/anonymize.ts`); there is a
  purge for materials and none for the `recordings` bucket. The retention rows
  written here say what is actually true ("while the teacher's account is open;
  ask us and we will delete it sooner") rather than claiming an automatic
  deletion that does not exist. Building that purge is real follow-up work.
- **Egress runs on a 2-OCPU box that warns it wants 4.** Room-composite
  recording was tested on it and completed cleanly at ~160% CPU
  (`docs/deployment/ORACLE_LIVEKIT_PRODUCTION.md`, "Confirmed findings"), and
  the box now carries both environments (D-94). Concurrent recordings are the
  scenario that warning is about; at one teacher they do not occur.
- **The recurring cost is the ASR, not LiveKit.** Self-hosted LiveKit and its
  Egress are open-source and run on hardware already paid for, so recording
  itself adds no per-minute vendor charge — that premise in the original
  turn-it-off decision is now genuinely gone. What does cost money is Deepgram,
  billed per audio-minute, and capture is **per speaker**, so a 60-minute class
  bills roughly 120 audio-minutes. R2 storage is small and, for the
  insights audio, transient by design.
- **The condition D-114 named no longer matched the business by the time this
  decision was taken.** The reasoning is not recorded here — see
  [D-159](./D-159.md).
