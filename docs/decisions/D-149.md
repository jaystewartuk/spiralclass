# D-149. A class for more than one person is still one booking: two people share one package, and a group of three or more will share one booking with a row per attendee

A class is always exactly one `Booking` row, whoever attends it. **Built:** a
package can be sold for two people who take every class together — one buyer,
one student account, one payment, `packages.seats = 2`, its own credit balance
and a price for two stored on the template
([packages](../features/packages.md)). **Scoped, not
built ([#16](https://github.com/jaystewartuk/spiralclass/issues/16)):** a group
class of three or more is one booking with a `BookingAttendee` row per student,
composed by the teacher from her own roster — no public seat inventory, no
waitlist, no student-created groups.

## Why

The database refuses the alternative. `bookings_no_overlap_active` and
`bookings_no_overlap_buffered` are GiST `EXCLUDE` constraints over the
teacher's scheduled intervals, so "four students, four rows, one slot" raises
`23P01` and the teacher would be told her own class took the slot. The
constraint is also right: her hour is spent once however many people are in it.
Keeping one row per class means the thirteen models keyed by `booking_id` —
the LiveKit room `class-<bookingId>` ([D-16](./D-16.md)), recording, summary,
content and materials, notes, assignments, auto-complete — keep working,
because they always described the class rather than the student.

A cheaper per-person price must not leak into dearer classes. The credit pool
was `(teacher, student identities, class length)`; without another axis a
discounted multi-person credit pays for a one-person class and the ledger does
it "correctly".

## Options considered

- **One booking per student in the same slot.** Rejected by the exclusion
  constraint, above.
- **For two people: each their own account and package, one booking with two
  attendees.** Right for strangers, wrong for a couple. It means dropping
  `bookings.student_id` across about 90 files, two sign-ups and two payments
  for two people at one table, and still no answer to "one of us pays for
  both".
- **For two people: keep the teacher's free-text "For two people" label.** What
  teachers were doing. The booking page never showed it, the ledger mixed the
  pools, checkout could not tell what it sold.
- **A price for two derived at checkout** (percent × one-person price). A charge
  would be a number nobody saw, and changing the percent would silently reprice
  every package for two. The percent only suggests a stored price.
- **Turning lesson insights off for every class for two.** Rejected in favour
  of the buyer confirming for the second person: a couple shares a room and
  usually a device. A package recorded by hand has no confirmation, so its
  classes are never captured.
- **An open, publicly bookable group class.** Deferred, not foreclosed: capacity
  on the booking, attendance per student and a format axis on credits make seat
  inventory additive later.

## What it cost

- The second person in a package for two has no account: cannot see the
  balance, book, or be messaged. Their consent is a checkbox the buyer ticks
  (`packages.partner_consent_at`), weaker than the per-pairing consent
  [D-22](./D-22.md) requires, and a minor as second person is not
  distinguished.
- AI materials still assume one student ([D-87](./D-87.md)); a class for two
  gets materials written for one.
- Templates labelled "for two" before this landed stay one-person packages
  until the teacher ticks the box, and then stay out of insights capture.
- Group classes will lose recording, transcript, insights, pronunciation, the
  pre-class brief and live captions. Consent is per teacher-and-student pair
  (`TeacherStudent`), the recording is one mixed audio composite of the room
  ([D-16](./D-16.md)) from which a non-consenting attendee cannot be removed,
  and `LessonAudioSpeaker` has only `student` and `teacher`.

## What it constrains

- **`packages.seats` is 1 or 2** (CHECK `packages_seats_one_or_two`, listed in
  `tests/migrations/invariants.integration.test.ts`). Three people is a group
  class; do not widen the CHECK to make one.
- **Credits never cross the seat axis.** The pool key is `(teacher, student
identities, class length, seats)` in
  [`lib/booking/credit-ledger.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/booking/credit-ledger.ts);
  guarded by "the seat axis" suite in
  [`tests/booking/credit-ledger.test.ts`](../../apps/web/tests/booking/credit-ledger.test.ts).
  A group class adds `format` to the same key.
- **A one-person agreed (grandfathered) price never prices a package for two**
  (`lib/payments/grandfathered-prices.ts`), and the percent never prices a
  charge (`priceForSeats` in
  [`two-person.ts`](../../packages/shared/src/two-person.ts)).
- **No insights capture for a class for two without `partner_consent_at`**
  (`lessonInsightsConsentOk`,
  [`lib/lesson-notes/consent.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/lesson-notes/consent.ts),
  tested in `tests/lesson-notes/consent.test.ts`).
- **Every multi-person offering is Pro**, through `entitlementsFor()`:
  `canSellForTwo` today, `canSellGroupClasses` when groups ship. Only switching
  a new one on is gated; after a downgrade, prices for two already on sale keep
  selling.
- **For groups, when built:** format is snapshotted on the booking and never
  derived from the attendee count (a group down to one attendee still spends a
  group credit); booking a group is all-or-nothing in one transaction; an
  attendee cancelling leaves the class under the ordinary 24-hour rule against
  her own package, the last one cancelling cancels it, and a teacher cancel
  refunds everyone; no-show is per attendee; `classes_used` counts attendee
  rows; only the teacher can move a group class and her move spends nobody's
  schedule-change budget; attendees are never named to each other; the call
  grid tops out at six (`GROUP_CLASS_MAX_CAPACITY`) with 1:1 geometry
  unchanged; per-attendee capture gating comes before the speaker axis, which
  comes before any capture pipeline. Both `bookings.student_id` and
  `bookings.package_id` are dropped in the same change, because there is no
  raw SQL in `apps/web/src` reading them and the compiler finds every site.

## How we got here

- **September 2026.** Group classes were scoped from the code, not from a request.
  Measured against `apps/web/src` on 2026-09-01: 91 files issued a `booking` query,
  61 of them also read a student id; 13 models were keyed by `booking_id`, and
  0 needed a per-attendee axis once the six capture surfaces were off. Every
  booking made in the first seven weeks — 155 — could be counted by hand. The
  record said nothing would be built until a teacher asked by name. The
  six-attendee ceiling is asserted from grid legibility, not load-tested on the
  2-OCPU LiveKit host.
- **2026-09-27.** A teacher's couple asked for an 8-class package together.
  The teacher had written "For two people" into a separate template's free-text
  subject; the booking page never showed it, the packages for two sat among the
  one-person ones and looked like the dearest thing on the page, and their
  credits shared a pool with one-person credits. Packages for two shipped
  (`20260927120000_packages_for_two`: three columns, four CHECKs), with a
  default suggesting percent of 150. The group-class shape stands for three or
  more.
- `LESSON_FORMATS` in `packages/shared/src/validators-core.ts` still has the
  single member `one_to_one`; adding `group` is the additive change its author
  predicted.
