# D-149. Group classes are one booking with many attendees — a closed roster, not a marketplace

**Status: Scoped, not built.** This decision fixes what a group class **is** in
SpiralClass, what it deliberately is not, and the four things that constrain
the shape whether anyone likes them or not. No code changed. The work it
implies lives on the private board, tagged `spiralclass`, and nowhere in this
tree — see [D-110](./D-110.md).

It also does not schedule the work. On precedent,
**nothing here is built until a teacher asks for it by name** or a second one
is turning students away for want of it. What this record buys is that when
that happens, the shape is already decided and the four constraints below are
already known, rather than discovered halfway through a migration.

## What was decided

**A group class is one `Booking` row with many attendees.** The booking keeps
its teacher, its start, its end, its buffer snapshot and both exclusion
constraints exactly as they are. A new `BookingAttendee` table carries
everything that was only ever true of one student: which package the credit
came off, whether that credit still counts, and whether she turned up.

**It is a closed roster, composed by the teacher.** She picks a slot, picks a
group offering, and picks the students from her own roster. There is no seat
inventory on the public booking page, no waitlist, no drop-in, and no way for
a student to create or join a group class on her own. Two sisters, a couple,
three colleagues from the same office — that is the product, and it is the
one a teacher can use the week it ships.

**The open, publicly bookable group class is deferred, not foreclosed.** Every
structural choice below is the one that makes seat inventory additive later:
capacity is already on the booking, attendance is already a per-student row,
and the credit pool already carries a format axis. What Phase 2 adds is a
public seat count and the concurrency around claiming the last one.

## Why one booking row, and not one per student

Not a preference. **The database rejects the other model today.**

`bookings_no_overlap_active` and `bookings_no_overlap_buffered` are GiST
`EXCLUDE` constraints over `(teacher_id, tstzrange(...))` where the status is
`scheduled` (see the portable-postgres baseline migration). Two scheduled
bookings for one teacher at one time raise Postgres error 23P01. So "four
students, four rows, same slot" does not merely duplicate data — it cannot be
inserted, and `isSlotConflictError` would report it to the teacher as "that
slot is taken", by her own class.

The constraint is also **right**, which is why the answer is to model with it
rather than around it: the teacher's hour is spent once whether one student or
six are in it. A group class occupies exactly one slot, so it is exactly one
booking.

The payoff is large and immediate. **Thirteen models hang off `booking_id`**,
and all thirteen keep working with no change at all, because every one of them
was always describing the class rather than the student: the LiveKit room key
`class-<bookingId>` ([D-16](./D-16.md)), the recording, the lesson summary,
the class content and attached library materials, the material-use records,
the live notes, the assignments, and the hourly auto-complete sweep.

## The one change that is forced: credits grow a format axis

Credits are fungible today within one pool, keyed
`(teacherId, studentIds, classDurationMin)` in `lib/booking/credit-ledger.ts`.
A group class is cheaper per student than a private one. If the pool key does
not change, a student buys ten discounted 50-minute group credits and spends
them on ten private 50-minute classes, and the ledger is behaving exactly as
designed while doing it.

So **the pool key becomes `(teacherId, studentIds, classDurationMin, format)`**,
where format is `one_to_one` or `group`. The ordering rule
(`compareCreditFifo`, soonest-to-expire first, never-expiring last) is
untouched; only the key widens. `PackageTemplate` grows `format` and
`capacity`; `Package` snapshots `format` at purchase, exactly as it already
snapshots price and currency; `Booking` snapshots `format` and `capacity` at
creation.

**Format is explicit on the booking, never derived from the attendee count.**
A group class down to its last attendee is still a group class and must still
spend a group credit — deriving it would silently re-price the class the
moment someone cancelled.

`LESSON_FORMATS` in `packages/shared/src/validators.ts` is already this enum.
[D-88](./D-88.md) created it with a single member and wrote down that the
extension would be additive **if** group classes ever shipped, naming as the
precondition "the `Booking` model itself to grow a real multi-student concept
first". This decision is that precondition, and the prompt-side work is the
three edits D-88 predicted: a `"group"` value, a branch in
`lessonFormatDirective()`, and resolving it from `Booking.format`. No call site
changes.

## What a group class is NOT in version one

### No recording, transcript, insights, pronunciation, brief or live captions

Off, and **enforced at the capture site rather than merely unbuilt**. This is
the finding that most changes the shape of the feature, and it is a hard
constraint, not an effort estimate.

**Consent is per teacher-and-student pair.** `insightsConsentAt`,
`captionsConsentAt`, `guardianConsentAt` and `isMinor` live on
`TeacherStudent` ([D-22](./D-22.md) and the captions review), and default to
off for every pairing. **[D-135](./D-135.md) made the class recording an
audio-only room composite** — one mixed file for the whole room. There is no
way to composite a group room without capturing an attendee who has not
consented, and no way to remove her from the mix afterwards. Per-track egress
can be gated per attendee; a composite cannot be.

Second, smaller, and independent: `LessonAudioSpeaker` has exactly two values,
`student` and `teacher`, and `LessonTranscript.utterances` labels every
utterance with one of them. A three-person transcript has nowhere to put the
third voice, and diarization is precisely what the two-file design was built
to avoid.

So the unfreeze order is fixed: **per-attendee capture gating first, then the
speaker axis, then the pipeline** — never the pipeline first.

### No split payment

Each attendee buys her own group package at the group price. There is no
one-payer-many-seats, no shared invoice, no family plan. It is a real request
and it will come; it needs a payer-versus-attendee split threaded through
`Package`, `Payment`, the discount and referral ledgers and the refund path,
and that is a decision of its own.

**Nothing about the money rail changes.** [D-143](./D-143.md) already made the
teacher merchant of record via direct charges on her own Stripe account, so a
group price is a `PackageTemplate` price and nothing more.

### No attendee-initiated reschedule

Moving a group class moves everyone, so **only the teacher can move one**, and
her move spends nobody's schedule-change budget. An attendee who cannot make
it cancels under the ordinary rules and books something else. This removes the
hardest concurrency problem in the feature — three students proposing three
different new times — for the cost of one sentence of copy.

## The rules

**Booking a group class is all-or-nothing.** Every attendee's credit is
claimed by `claimNextCredit` inside the same transaction as the booking
insert. A partial group is never written — a class with three of the four
students the teacher chose is worse than an error message.

**An attendee cancelling leaves the class; the class still runs.** The timing
rule is the existing one, unchanged, applied per attendee against her own
package: `lib/cancellation/classify.ts` already takes a booking and a package
and returns a timing, so it needs no edit. At least 24 hours ahead returns her
credit and spends one unit of her own package's pooled schedule-change budget;
under 24 hours forfeits the class and spends nothing, because a penalty is not
a move.

**The last attendee cancelling cancels the class.** Forced by the same
exclusion constraint as everything else: an attendee-less booking still holds
the teacher's slot and would block a real booking from landing on it. The
teacher is notified rather than merely finding a hole in her calendar.

**A teacher cancel cascades to every attendee**, refunds each in full
regardless of timing, and writes one `Override` row against the booking, as it
does today. Blocking a date range keeps working unchanged, because it cancels
through that same teacher-cancel path.

**No-show becomes per attendee.** `BookingAttendee.attendance` is
`expected`, `attended` or `no_show`; `BookingStatus.no_show` keeps its
existing meaning of nobody came at all.

**`Package.classesUsed` re-anchors, and stays exactly as true.** Its
definition moves from counting bookings to counting attendee rows:
`classes_used = COUNT(booking_attendees WHERE counts_against_package)`. The
per-package field-comparison guard under the row lock is the race guard, as
before.

**Attendees are not named to each other.** Not a judgement call —
`docs/features/user-roles.md` already states it exhaustively: a student can
see her own bookings, packages and settings with the teachers she is linked
to, "never another student's data". So her class shows the teacher, the time,
and "4 places, 1 free", and never a name. There is no teacher toggle in
version one; a closed group of siblings obviously knows itself, but the
platform cannot tell that group from a stranger's, and the reversible default
is the quiet one.

**Selling group classes is Pro.** A new `canSellGroupClasses` entitlement
resolved by `entitlementsFor()`, alongside the live surfaces the call already
sits behind ([D-16](./D-16.md)). It multiplies what a teacher earns per hour,
which is the clearest thing on the platform to charge for. The Free
`studentLimit` of 3 does not change — a group class does not change how many
students she has.

## The interface

**Composing a class is slot, then offering, then attendees**, in that order,
because the slot is the scarce thing and the roster is filtered by what the
slot can hold.

**The attendee picker shows each student's balance in the group pool inline,
before she picks.** The failure this flow exists to design out is choosing
four students and learning at submit that one has no eligible credit. A
student with no credit is **listed and disabled with the reason shown**, next
to a one-tap way to record a package — never hidden, because a missing name
reads as a bug and sends her to support.

**Seats remaining is a live count in `aria-live="polite"`**, so it is
announced as she selects rather than only drawn.

**The class detail page makes the attendee list the primary object**, not a
footnote under the class content: real list semantics, one row per attendee,
each carrying attendance state and which package the credit came off. Single
column below 1024 and a two-column split above it, per [D-141](./D-141.md) —
the roster beside the class content, never a third column.

**The call stage becomes a grid, and 1:1 does not move.** One participant
fills the stage; two keep exactly today's big-plus-floating geometry from
`lib/video/stage-layout.ts`, unchanged, so the only shape anyone uses today
cannot regress; three or four take a two-by-two; five or six a three-by-two.
The ceiling is **six** (`GROUP_CLASS_MAX_CAPACITY`, in shared config): past
that a language class is a webinar, and six is the largest grid that stays
legible at a phone width.

**The active speaker is marked with a border, never by resizing tiles.** A
layout that reflows every time somebody speaks is unusable in a class where
people take turns by design. `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the swap
transition entirely.

**Names are always drawn on tiles, never revealed on hover** — there is no
hover on the touch devices half of this runs on, and a hover-only label is
invisible to a screen reader.

**The call ships a text participant list** beside the grid for screen readers
and for anyone who cannot parse six simultaneous video tiles. Every state that
carries meaning — class full, you cancelled, class cancelled, someone did not
turn up — has a text equivalent and is never signalled by colour alone.

**The calendar chip needs a label, not a name.** `CalendarEvent.title` in
`components/calendar/event.ts` is one name by contract — the student's on the
teacher's calendar, the teacher's on the student's. A group class has no single
student, so on the teacher's side it must render the offering and the count
("Conversation Club, 4") rather than silently picking one of four attendees.
The student's side already shows the teacher and stays correct. Note that
[D-141](./D-141.md)'s 15px floor makes this tight: the month cell was rebuilt
on 2026-09-01 to fit exactly one 15px chip line, so the longer label has to be
measured against `min-h-cell` rather than assumed to fit.

**Live notes stay as they are.** Teacher cues remain teacher-private; the
student-audience note becomes class-level and shared, which is what a group
class wants anyway. `NoteAudience` is unchanged. Per-attendee private
instructions are deferred.

**Every new string goes through the shared catalog** in all three locales
(es-MX, en, fr) via `getT` or `useT`, with interpolation for the seat counts,
which the catalog already supports. **New components use tokens and the
existing `Badge` and `Alert` variants**, so `pnpm design:drift` does not grow
— the 107 raw palette utilities the design baseline counted are the ones that
render light-on-light in dark mode, and this feature must not add the 108th.

## Blast radius, measured

Measured against `apps/web/src` on 2026-09-01:

| What                                      | Count |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----- |
| files issuing a `booking` query           | 91    |
| of those, files also reading a student id | 61    |
| models keyed by `booking_id`              | 13    |
| models that need a new per-attendee axis  | 0     |

In prose, because the numbers are the argument: **91 files issue a booking
query and 61 of them also read a student id**, so removing
`bookings.student_id` is a wide change. **Thirteen models hang off
`booking_id` and none of them needs a per-student axis**, because the six that
would have (transcript, audio, insights, pronunciation, brief, and the
captions path) are the six this decision turns off.

**Both `bookings.student_id` and `bookings.package_id` are dropped, in one
change, not kept alongside the new table.** Two representations of one fact is
the failure this codebase has diagnosed repeatedly — the flat custom price
that could not say which package it meant, two payment rails for one bank
transfer ([D-145](./D-145.md)), a second copy of a commitment that rots while
the board moves on. Expand-and-contract exists to protect a fleet of readers
during a rollout; there is one deploy, one operator, and a table small enough
that every booking made in seven weeks could be counted by hand (155).
Staging it would buy nothing and leave a permanent ambiguity about which
column to trust.

One shot is safe **here specifically** because the compiler finds every site:
there is no raw SQL anywhere in `src` that reads `bookings.student_id`, so
there is no residue outside the type system. On a codebase with raw queries
this would be the wrong call.

## Unresolved risks

- **Nobody has asked for this.** No teacher has requested a group class by
  name; the scope is derived from the code and from what a language teacher's
  business obviously wants, not from a measured request. An entire client was
  once frozen on exactly that distinction. Building this before the ask would
  be the parity tax under a different name.

- **The credit-format split is invisible until it bites.** A teacher who
  prices a group class carelessly, or a student holding both pools, will meet
  a "you have no eligible classes" message while a balance is plainly visible
  on screen. `summarizeCreditPools` must show the two pools separately and
  label them, or the split reads as a bug.

- **Turning the insights pipeline off for group classes is a permanent-feeling
  gap.** A teacher who has come to rely on the pre-class brief loses it for
  half her timetable, and the reason (a consent model plus an audio composite)
  is not visible to her. The copy has to say so at the point she creates the
  class, not in a settings page.

- **The six-attendee ceiling is asserted, not measured.** It comes from grid
  legibility and from what a language class is, not from a load test of the
  self-hosted LiveKit box, which is a 2-OCPU machine also running Redis and
  Caddy ([D-135](./D-135.md)). Six simultaneous publishers on it is untested.
