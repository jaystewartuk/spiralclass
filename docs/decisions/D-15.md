# D-15. Live notes panel: student-visibility by class window, present-mode gated Pro (2026-06-23)

**Status: Active.** Both decisions have now shipped on web — the class-window
visibility model + schema (phase 1a), and the Pro gating of the live surfaces:
present mode (phase 1b) and the realtime student panel (phase 1c) are both gated
via `gateProFeature(teacher.id, "lesson_notes")` against the `canUseLiveNotes`
entitlement, while authoring stays free on every tier. The phase-1c RLS policy
(`lesson_notes_student_select`) enforces both halves of decision 1 in the
database. See `live-notes-panel.md`.

**What was decided.** Two policy questions the live notes design can't pick by
feel:

1. **Student-visibility model = a time window, not a "go live" toggle.**
   Student-audience notes (`lesson_notes.audience = 'student'`) are visible to
   the student only from `scheduledStart − 15m` through `scheduledEnd + buffer`.
   Teacher-audience cues are never visible to the student at any time. There is
   no teacher-controlled live/publish switch in v1.

2. **Pro gating = authoring free, live surfaces Pro.** Creating/editing notes
   (both audiences) is available on Free. **Present mode** (the teleprompter view)
   and the **live student panel** are gated via `gateProFeature(teacher.id,
"lesson_notes")`, alongside the existing `"materials"` gate. The step-2 AI
   summary will also be Pro.

**Why.**

- _Window over toggle:_ it adds no new state and reuses the exact
  `materialSendTimeElapsed()` mechanism that already governs when class materials
  appear (`lib/materials/timing.ts`), so a teacher can prep cues days ahead
  without a leak and without a switch to forget to flip. A manual "go live"
  toggle is the kind of stateful control that's easy to leave in the wrong
  position mid-class; defer it until there's evidence the fixed window is too
  rigid.
- _Authoring free / live Pro:_ mirrors how class-materials scheduling is already
  a Pro feature, and keeps the two `CLAUDE.md` guardrails intact — notes touch no
  payment rail (so this never paywalls _getting paid_), and on downgrade
  over-entitlement notes go **read-only**, never deleted. Putting the paywall on
  the live/present experience (not on authoring) lets every teacher build lesson
  plans while reserving the in-class magic — the actual conversion hook — for Pro.

**Relationship to D-14 (teacher-private student notes).** That table
(`student_notes`) is a _per-student_ private memo on the roster; this one
(`lesson_notes`) is a _per-booking_ cue list with a student-facing column. They
share the "teacher writes a note" shape but are distinct surfaces with distinct
audiences — neither subsumes the other, and the names are kept separate on
purpose.

**What it constrains.**

- The `lesson_notes` schema needs no `publishedAt`/`isLive` column — visibility
  is derived from the booking's schedule. Don't add one without revisiting this.
- The student read paths must apply the window server-side, the way the booking
  read already gates materials, not in the client.
- The Supabase Realtime RLS policy (phase 1c) must enforce both halves of
  decision 1: `audience = 'student'` **and** the student owns the booking. The
  time window is a product gate; RLS is the security gate. They are not the same
  control and both are required.

**Unresolved risks.**

- The fixed 15-minute pre-class window is a guess. If teachers want cues visible
  earlier (e.g. as pre-reading) we either widen it or revisit the toggle — at
  which point this decision is superseded, not edited.
- `buffer` after `scheduledEnd` is borrowed loosely from the teacher's
  inter-class buffer; the exact post-class visibility tail isn't pinned and may
  need its own constant rather than reusing `bufferMin`.
- Gating present mode but not authoring means a Free teacher can write a full
  cue list and then hit the wall at "Go live". That nudge must read as an
  upgrade prompt, not a dead end (reuse `upgradeNudge()`), or it feels like a
  bait-and-switch.

---

**Update (2026-07-15) — standalone present mode retired.** The full-screen
teleprompter "present mode" screen and its entry buttons (web `lesson-notes-panel`
"Go live") were removed. Video calls now happen in-app and the teacher's private
cues overlay directly on the live call, so a separate teleprompter screen was
redundant. The cue authoring surface (teacher-audience lesson notes),
the cues endpoint/types, and the Pro gate are all unchanged — only the standalone
screen and its `present.*` copy (except the shared `present.back` label) are gone.
This moots the last "Go live wall" risk above: there is no longer a "Go live"
entry point to gate.
