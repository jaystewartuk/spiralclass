# D-125. Make SpiralClass a student-acquisition assistant, not only a scheduler

**What changed.** SpiralClass now has a first-class student-acquisition system,
built on top of the acquisition pieces that already existed rather than beside
them. Six things landed together.

- **A command centre, "Consigue alumnos" (Get students).** A top-level
  destination, `/dashboard/get-students`, which answers one question: what should I do today to get my next
  student. It shows today's two prepared actions, then the rest of the week,
  then the numbers, in that order.
- **A weekly plan.** A pure function in `@spiralclass/shared`
  (`marketing/planner.ts`) decides the week's mix from real signals: which
  communities have produced students, which have gone quiet, which have never
  been tried, what the teacher already posted where, how many minutes a week she
  says she has, and whether there is a genuine referral moment. The plan is
  persisted as `MarketingPlan` plus its `MarketingActivity` rows.
- **Content generation.** Claude writes each post from the teacher's own facts,
  in a variant specific to the destination platform and the community's stated
  promotion rules. The existing D-123 image pipeline generates the picture where
  one helps.
- **Communities, generalised.** The Facebook-group row a teacher already saved
  is now a marketing community with a platform, a promotion policy and an
  audience note. Same table, same ids, same already-posted links.
- **An acquisition ledger.** `AcquisitionEvent` records visit, enquiry, booking
  and purchase, each carrying the first-touch attribution that produced it, so
  the product itself can answer "which community produced a paying student".
- **A results screen and an observation engine.** Channel, community and content
  performance, plus findings labelled observed, pattern or suggestion, each
  gated behind a sample-size and effect-size floor.

**Why it likely changed.** Every ingredient already existed and none of them
closed the loop. First-touch attribution was captured in the `ap_src` cookie and
sent to PostHog and nowhere else, so the question the launch was posted to answer
was answerable only to someone holding a PostHog login, never to the teacher and
never to the software. Teachers could save Facebook groups, and a fortnightly
cron told them to post in those groups again, without ever saying what to post.
The image generator produced a picture with no text to put around it. The Crecer
checklist was five fixed onboarding steps that stopped being useful the moment
they were done. Each piece was real; together they were a set of parts, and the
teacher was still doing all the thinking.

The strategic reframing is that SpiralClass should not only help a teacher manage
the students she has. It should help her get the next one. That is a different
product, and it needs the loop closed at both ends: something has to decide what
to do, and something has to measure what happened.

**What was affected.**

_Data model._ Four new tables and one generalised in place.
`TeacherMarketingProfile` holds only what cannot be inferred: who she wants to
reach, where they are, what makes her different, and how many minutes a week she
has. `MarketingPlan` is one week of prepared work. `MarketingActivity` is one
prepared action and its tracked link, and it is also the campaign entity — a
separate campaign table was rejected because for one independent teacher a
campaign IS the activity, and the grouping she actually thinks in is the week.
`AcquisitionEvent` is the append-only funnel ledger.

`teacher_share_groups` gained `platform`, `promo_policy`, `audience_note` and
`archived_at`. The table was NOT renamed, deliberately: renaming it would churn
the API surface, the `SocialPreview.shareGroupId` relation and the
`utm_content` slugs derived from these ids in links that are live in real
community feeds today, and it would buy nothing a comment cannot. Every existing
row backfills exactly, because every existing row genuinely is a Facebook group.

_Attribution._ `/g/<code>` is the new tracked link. It stamps the same first-touch
`ap_src` cookie the middleware would have written from UTM parameters, then
redirects to a clean `/b/<slug>`. Links posted before this change, carrying the
old `utm_content` share-group slug, still resolve — `matchShareGroupBySlug` is
reused rather than replaced, and the two schemes coexist on one teacher's
history. A short link exists because a booking URL with four tracking parameters
reads as an advertisement in a community feed, and teachers were editing the
parameters off, which silently destroyed the attribution.

A first-party visitor cookie, `ap_vid`, was added so a visit can be tied to the
enquiry it produced and so unique visitors are distinguishable from page loads.
It is HMAC-ed under `SESSION_SECRET` before storage. No IP address, no user
agent, and no referrer path is stored — only a referring origin, which the
existing attribution module already capped and charset-limited.

_Notifications._ The fortnightly Facebook-groups nudge cron was retired and
replaced by a Monday `student_acquisition_plan_teacher` send, which builds the
week's plan and names its first action. The old template definition stays so
historical notification rows still render; only the cron and its handler were
removed. The nudge remains suppressible under the same "growth" preference
category.

_Surfaces merged._ `/dashboard/share-groups` now permanently redirects to
`/dashboard/get-students/communities`, which absorbed the per-community social
previews from D-123. The two were always one decision: where do I post, and what
does my link look like when I do.

**Automation boundary.** The system does everything around participating in a
community and nothing that constitutes participating in one. It discovers nothing
by scraping, posts nothing anywhere, and sends nothing to anyone on the teacher's
behalf. Facebook removed Groups publishing in 2024 and Reddit treats unsolicited
promotion as spam, so a product that automated the posting would be handing
teachers a ban rather than a marketing department. The promotion policy on each
community is the enforcement point: a promotional action is never planned into a
community whose rules are prohibited or merely unconfirmed, and `unknown` is
treated as not allowed, because silence is not consent.

**Honesty boundary.** The planner and the observation engine are pure functions
over real counts, not model calls. Claude writes words; it never decides what a
number means. Findings are labelled observed, pattern or suggestion, and a
comparison is only stated once both sides clear a visit floor and the gap clears
an effect floor. Below that the screen says there is not enough data yet.
Content generation is bounded the same way: a testimonial post cannot be planned
without a published testimonial, a package offer cannot be planned without a
package, and the prompt instructs the model to quote verbatim or not at all.

**Unresolved risks.**

- **Attribution is first-touch and cookie-based.** A visitor who blocks cookies,
  switches device, or arrives more than 90 days after first contact is recorded
  as direct. The results screen therefore under-credits every channel to some
  degree, and consistently rather than randomly, so comparisons between channels
  stay meaningful even where absolute numbers do not.
- **Generation cost is unbounded per teacher beyond a rate limit.** Content
  generation is throttled at 20 per 10 minutes per teacher, and image generation
  reuses the existing D-123 monthly cap. There is no monthly cap on text
  generation yet. If usage becomes material, the cap belongs in
  `subscriptions/config.ts` next to the others, resolved through
  `entitlementsFor()`.
- **The planner has never seen a teacher with fifty communities.** Its
  round-robin bounds itself at three passes, so it degrades to fewer actions
  rather than looping, but the ranking has not been exercised at that scale.
- **Platform rules change.** The promotion policy per community is what the
  teacher recorded, and nothing verifies it. A community that tightens its rules
  keeps whatever she last said, until she changes it.
- **The weekly nudge builds a plan per eligible teacher inside one cron run.**
  It is capped at 500 teachers per run and each plan is a handful of queries; at
  a much larger roster this wants to become a fan-out rather than a loop.
