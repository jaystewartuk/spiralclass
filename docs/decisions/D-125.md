# D-125. Student acquisition is an assistant inside the product: it plans and drafts, the teacher posts, and no model decides what the numbers mean

SpiralClass helps a teacher get her own next student from inside the app — a
weekly plan decided by a pure function
([`planner.ts`](../../packages/shared/src/marketing/planner.ts)), posts and
images drafted by the platform's models from her own facts, and an
append-only attribution ledger (`AcquisitionEvent`) — and it never posts,
sends or scrapes on her behalf, and is not a marketplace. A generated image
carries no text: the model draws a background, and every character on the card
is rendered by our own route (`/api/og/social-preview/[id]`).

Behaviour lives in [student acquisition](../features/student-acquisition.md).

## Why

Demand, not operations, was the bottleneck: the first teacher had spare
capacity and not enough students, and students arrived only through a
hand-shared `/b/<slug>` link. By August 2026 every part of a loop existed and
none closed it — first-touch attribution reached PostHog and nowhere else, a
fortnightly cron told her to post in her saved groups without saying what, and
the image generator produced a picture with no text around it.

Two outside facts bound the shape. Facebook removed Groups publishing in 2024,
and Reddit treats unsolicited promotion as spam, so automating the posting
would hand teachers a ban. And image models fail on exactly the accented
characters a language teacher's post needs; a misspelt Spanish meme posted by a
Spanish teacher discredits the expertise she is advertising.

## Options considered

- **A separate acquisition app** — a second frontend on the same Postgres and a
  second deploy pipeline, for no isolation gain; acquisition is inseparable from
  the booking page, checkout and auth.
- **A marketplace or directory** — pays off only at dozens of teachers and
  hundreds of students, and reverses the product's no-marketplace stance.
- **Automating the posting** — a ban, for the reasons above.
- **A model reading the results** — it would state conclusions the counts do not
  support. The planner and the observation engine
  ([`insights.ts`](../../packages/shared/src/marketing/insights.ts)) are pure
  functions with floors: no comparison below 20 visits a side, no claim below 2
  students, no "better" below a 1.5× ratio.
- **The image on the booking page rather than the share link** — one meme would
  become the preview in every context she has. The image belongs to a placement
  (`SocialPreview`), resolved from the link's `utm_content` tag, else her
  default, else the built-in card.
- **Letting the model render the caption** — misspellings, and every caption
  edit a paid regeneration.
- **Baking the composite** — a caption edit becomes a re-upload plus orphan
  cleanup. The background is stored; the card is rendered on demand, and its
  URL carries `?v=<updatedAt>` because Facebook and WhatsApp cache a URL's
  first scrape.
- **A purpose × style matrix** — the two were ~95% correlated for this audience;
  one axis, `angle`, named so a second axis is additive later.
- **The public Gemini API key, OpenAI, fal.ai, Replicate** — each gates calls on
  a prepaid balance. Vertex AI bills through the ordinary postpaid Cloud Billing
  account ([`gemini-image.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/ai/gemini-image.ts)).
- **One hardcoded image brief** — produced the same picture for everyone (see
  below). The brief now has three authors: fixed platform rules, the teacher's
  `meme_brief` and style, and the community's `meme_brief`.

## What it cost

- Attribution is first-touch and cookie-based. A visitor who blocks cookies,
  switches device or returns after 90 days is recorded as direct, so every
  channel is under-credited — consistently, so comparisons hold where absolute
  numbers do not. We count landings, not clicks on the post.
- Crawler behaviour cannot be tested from the suite. What is pinned:
  `tests/social-preview/og-metadata.test.ts` and `card-route.test.ts`. The
  Facebook Sharing Debugger, WhatsApp and LinkedIn's Post Inspector need a human.
- A community's promotion policy and free-text rules are what the teacher
  recorded; nothing verifies them. The frequency rule counts only posts she
  marks done.
- One live draft per community; image rotation (eight framings, five registers)
  makes repetition less likely and nothing measures whether images got better.
- Text generation has a rate limit and no monthly cap. The code limits it to
  15 per 10 minutes per teacher (`lib/marketing/content.ts`); the original
  record said 20.

## What it constrains

- **Never add a code path that posts, sends or scrapes on a teacher's behalf.**
- **`unknown` promotion policy means not allowed.** `allowsDirectPromotion()` in
  [`channels.ts`](../../packages/shared/src/marketing/channels.ts) admits only
  `open` and `limited`; `planner.test.ts` pins "never schedules a promotional
  action into a prohibited community" and "…where the rules are unconfirmed".
  A promotion window is advisory, never a block, and is computed in her
  timezone ([`promotion-rules.ts`](../../packages/shared/src/marketing/promotion-rules.ts)).
- **Claude writes words only.** A testimonial post needs a published
  testimonial, a package offer needs a package, and quotes are verbatim or
  absent. Testimonial rules are [D-151](./D-151.md).
- **The image model renders no text.** `tests/social-preview/prompt.test.ts`
  pins "forbids every form of in-image text"; the shocked-face failure is an
  explicit negative constraint in
  [`social-preview/prompt.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/social-preview/prompt.ts).
  The teacher never sees or writes a raw prompt.
- **`teacher_share_groups` is not renamed.** Its ids feed `utm_content` slugs in
  links live in real feeds; `/g/<code>` links and old `utm_content` links both
  resolve (`matchShareGroupBySlug`).
- **`ap_vid` is HMAC'd under `SESSION_SECRET`**; no IP, user agent or referrer
  path is stored — only a capped referring origin.
- **Getting students is never paywalled.** Lead capture, testimonials and image
  uploads are free on every tier. AI images are 3 a month on Free and 20 on Pro
  ([`social-preview.ts`](../../packages/shared/src/social-preview.ts)). A text
  cap, if added, goes through `entitlementsFor()`.
- **Image deletion resolves the `Restrict` foreign keys** rather than forcing
  past them: a post marked done blocks it, a draft's reference is cleared, a
  placement falls back to the standard card, and the bytes go last.
- **One default preview per teacher is an application-layer guarantee.** A
  partial unique index cannot be expressed in the Prisma schema and would read
  as drift.
- **Production calls Vertex as the Cloud Run runtime identity**, `web-runtime`,
  whose only project role is `roles/aiplatform.user` —
  `tests/config/cloudrun-deploy.test.ts` pins "gives the runtime identity exactly
  one project role, the Vertex AI caller". A service-account key
  (`GEMINI_VERTEX_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_KEY_BASE64`) is the off-GCP path only and wins
  while set; retiring the production copy is the operator's: remove it, redeploy,
  confirm "Generate with AI" works, then delete that service account.

## How we got here

- **June 2026.** Phase 1: lead capture and teacher-authored testimonials on
  `/b/<slug>`, a leads inbox, a typed PostHog funnel. In-app, amplifying the
  teacher, free on every tier.
- **August 2026.** Social previews: an image per share link, uploaded or
  generated, text rendered by Satori. Free 3, Pro 20 — about four or five posts
  with three or four regenerations each, on the order of a dollar a month.
- **August 2026, undated.** The acquisition assistant: the "Get
  students" command centre, the weekly plan, content generation, communities
  generalised from saved Facebook groups, the `AcquisitionEvent` ledger and the
  results screen. The fortnightly groups cron was replaced by a Monday plan
  notification (capped at 500 teachers a run). `/dashboard/share-groups`
  redirects to the communities screen.
- **2026-08-24.** The first real production generation returned `429
RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED — "Your prepayment credits are depleted"`: the public API
  key needs a prepay balance a linked billing account does not satisfy, and
  buying credits needs a human at a payment screen. The same model moved to
  Vertex with a signed-JWT service-account key. Location `global`, because
  `us-central1` returned 404 for this model, found by trying it; it stays
  overridable (`GEMINI_VERTEX_LOCATION`).
- **Before September 2026, undated.** Generated memes were reliably a shocked
  face. The cause was our own brief, shared by every teacher, asking for "an exaggerated,
  instantly readable facial expression". It was deleted, the failure became a
  negative constraint, the brief gained teacher and community authors, and a
  variation index seeded on her monthly image count rotates framing and style.
  Communities gained structured rules (`promo_weekdays`, `promo_every_days`,
  `promo_links_allowed`) and free-text `promo_notes` fed to the prompt.
- **2026-09-24.** Production now running on Cloud Run ([D-184](./D-184.md)),
  Vertex tokens come from the metadata server; the stored key was kept only for
  off-GCP use. Google's Workload Identity Federation, the earlier answer to the
  long-lived key, was never needed.
