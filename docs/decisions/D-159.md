# D-159. This repository publishes what was built, not the operator's legal and financial position

**What changed.** The regulatory and tax material is out of the tree.
[D-147](./D-147.md) — the platform's position under UK digital platform
reporting, payment services regulation and SCA — is a tombstone with its one
engineering finding kept. The operator's personal position is out of
[D-58](./D-58.md), the FCA and PERG framing is out of [D-143](./D-143.md)'s
liability paragraph, and [D-128](./D-128.md) records the privacy-notice rewrite
without the quotable form of what it was rewritten from. Four compliance
passages had already gone the same way on 2026-09-04.

## The rule

> **What was built and why it was built that way is published. What the operator
> owes, to whom, and under which regime is not.**

**This is the second half of [D-158](./D-158.md)'s rule and it has the same
shape.** D-158 says publish the shape and never the account. This one says
publish the engineering and never the operator's exposure. Both exist because
the repository is public and one person carries everything in it personally.

## Why this reverses a considered decision

**The pre-publication plan argued at length for keeping every decision record
unedited**, including the compliance passages, on three grounds: nobody is
identifiable, each record is the fix rather than the failure, and a log with the
uncomfortable parts removed is a worse artefact than a candid one.

⚠️ **Those arguments are still right about the engineering and were wrong about
the operator.** They defend a decision record that says _we shipped this, we
found it was wrong, here is what we did_ — which is the strongest evidence in
this repository and stays. They do not extend to a paragraph about one
person's own regulatory and tax obligations. **Nothing about
that is an engineering decision, and no reader looking for how the platform
works is worse off without it.**

**The asymmetry is the whole argument.** The candour costs a hostile reader
nothing to weaponise and buys a technical reader real signal; the tax and
regulatory position buys the technical reader nothing at all and hands the
hostile one a specific, dated, personal exposure. **When the two came apart,
the plan had treated them as one thing.**

## Where the line falls

| Published                                                                                          | Not published                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A decision, its constraint, and what it cost                                                       | What the operator personally owes or is liable for               |
| A mistake found and fixed, in the author's own words                                               | The operator's own tax and regulatory obligations                |
| Why an architecture changed, including the risk it removed                                         | The regulatory classification of the architecture it replaced    |
| The privacy layer, the supplier register, the lawful bases the product states                      | A quotable admission that a live document was legally wrong      |
| That the entity is UK-established, where it explains a country gate, a currency or a governing law | Its legal form, and what that means for whoever stands behind it |

⚠️ **The last row is the one to get right.** The entity's shape is
load-bearing: it is why `SUPPORTED_CONNECT_COUNTRIES` looks as it does, why
billing is GBP, why the privacy layer is UK GDPR. **The person behind it is
not**, and the two are separable in every place they appeared.

## Removed, and said so

**A tombstone rather than a silent deletion.** D-147 says what it covered and
why it is gone. **A record that quietly shrinks is the dishonesty the original
argument was protecting against; a record that says "this was removed, here is
what it was about, here is the rule" is not.**

⚠️ **No check enforces this one.** D-158's gate works because an identifier has
a shape; a sentence about a tax position does not. **This is a rule a reader
applies**, and the one question to ask of anything new in `docs/` is: _does this
tell someone how the platform was built, or does it tell them something about
its operator they could use?_
