# D-188. A package can be sold for two people who take every class together — one buyer, one account, its own credit balance

**Status.** Active. Narrows [D-149](./D-149.md) for the one multi-person shape
a teacher has actually asked for; D-149 stays the plan for group classes of
three or more.

**What changed.** A package template can carry a **price for two**
(`package_templates.two_person_price_minor_units`, and a non-card twin), and a
purchased package records how many people it is for (`packages.seats`, 1 or
2). Two people who take every class together — a couple, two siblings — buy
**one** package, paid **once** by **one** of them, on **one** student account.
Each class is one booking and uses one class from the package. Nothing about
`Booking`, the call, or the exclusion constraints changes.

- **The booking page** shows a "Just me / Two of us" switch when a teacher
  sells anything for two. The list for two shows the price for two and what
  one person pays for one class — the figure a couple compares against a
  one-person package. `?seats=2` opens it on "Two of us".
- **Checkout** charges `priceForSeats(template, seats, rail)`
  (`packages/shared/src/two-person.ts`). A one-person grandfathered price
  never prices a package for two. The buyer must confirm the second person
  agrees to the class terms they choose; that confirmation is
  `packages.partner_consent_at`.
- **Credits are separate balances.** The credit pool key is
  `(teacher, student identities, class length, seats)`. A one-person credit
  never pays for a class for two, and a credit for two never pays for a class
  for one.
- **Lesson-insights capture** for a class paid from a package for two needs
  the buyer's own consent **and** `partner_consent_at`
  (`lessonInsightsConsentOk`, `lib/lesson-notes/consent.ts`). A package for two
  that the teacher recorded by hand has no confirmation, so its classes are
  never captured.
- **The price for two is stored, not derived.** The teacher sets a percent
  (`teachers.two_person_price_percent`, default 150 — the second person pays
  half). It only **suggests** a price in the package editor. Every charge is a
  number stored on the template that the teacher saw.
- **Selling for two is Pro** (`canSellForTwo`, the entitlements resolver), as
  D-149 makes every multi-person offering. Only switching a **new** one on is
  gated: after a downgrade, prices for two already on the booking page keep
  selling.

**Why.** A teacher's couple asked for an 8-class package together. Before
this there was no way to say it: the teacher wrote "For two people" into the
free-text `subject` of a separate template. The booking page never showed that
label. The packages for two sat between the packages for one, ordered by class
count. The only per-class figure made each package for two look like the
dearest thing on the page. The label also put a couple's credits in the same
pool as one-person credits, so a cheaper credit could pay for a dearer class.
D-149's premise — "nothing here is built until a teacher asks for it by name"
— stopped being true.

**What was considered and rejected.**

- **D-149 as written: each partner their own account and package, one booking
  with many attendees.** Right for a group of strangers, wrong for a couple.
  It drops `bookings.student_id` across ~90 files. It makes two people who
  share a kitchen table sign up twice and pay twice. And it still has no
  answer for the case in hand, "one of us pays for both". D-149 itself called
  that payer-versus-attendee split "a decision of its own". This is that
  decision, for two people only.
- **Keep the free-text label and style it.** It fixes the booking page and
  leaves everything else wrong. The ledger still mixes the pools. Checkout
  still cannot tell what it sold. The receipt still cannot say it.
- **A derived price (percent × one-person price at checkout).** A charge would
  be a number no one looked at, and a percent change would silently reprice
  every package for two. Stored prices plus a suggesting percent keep the
  convenience without that.
- **Turning insights off for every class for two** (D-149's rule for groups).
  Rejected in favour of the buyer confirming for the second person. The pair
  shares a room and, usually, a device. A couple's own class is the case where
  one person can speak for the other. A package recorded by hand has no such
  confirmation, so it falls back to D-149's rule.

**What it constrains.**

- `seats` is 1 or 2 (`packages_seats_one_or_two`). A third person is a group
  class and goes through D-149. Do not widen this CHECK to make a group class.
- Never let a one-person credit pay for a class for two, or a one-person
  agreed price price a package for two. Both are money leaks that look like
  features.
- Never capture insights for a class for two without `partner_consent_at`.
- The percent never prices a charge.

**What was affected.** `apps/web/prisma/migrations/20260927120000_packages_for_two`
(three columns, four CHECKs), `packages/shared/src/two-person.ts`, the
entitlements resolver, `lib/payments/{start-checkout,grandfathered-prices,instruments}.ts`,
`lib/booking/{credit-ledger,book-package-slot}.ts`,
`lib/video/call-recording.ts`, `lib/lesson-notes/consent.ts`, the package
editor (`/settings/templates`), the booking page and buy flow (`/b/[slug]`,
`/b/[slug]/buy`), the portal repurchase and booking pages, and the teacher's
record/edit-package forms.

**Trade-offs accepted, and unresolved risks.**

- **The second person has no account.** They cannot see the balance, book a
  class, or be messaged. For a couple that is fine. For two unrelated people
  it is not, and that is D-149's product.
- **Consent for the second person is vouched, not given.** It is a checkbox
  the buyer ticks. It is recorded and required, but it is weaker than the
  per-pairing consent D-22 requires of the buyer. A minor as the second
  person is not distinguished.
- **AI materials still assume one-to-one** ([D-88](./D-88.md)). A class for
  two gets materials written for one student. Adding a pair directive is
  additive and was left out of this change.
- **Existing "for two" templates stay as they are.** Anything sold under the
  free-text label before this landed is a one-person package in the ledger
  until the teacher edits it and ticks "This package is for two people". Its
  classes then stay out of insights capture, because it has no buyer
  confirmation.
