# D-133. The framing is global, the name stays `AgendaProfe`, and the reset's market premise is withdrawn (2026-08-27)

**Status: Superseded by [D-138](./D-138.md) (2026-08-29).** The naming half of
this decision was reversed by owner's instruction; the framing half — that the
product is global and the reset's Spanish-market premise is withdrawn — still
stands and is what D-138 builds on. Everything below is left exactly as written
on 2026-08-27. Amends the naming call in the **2026-08-23 strategic reset**, a
document held outside this repository. Touches no code. Depends on nothing; constrains
[D-72](./D-72.md)/[D-73](./D-73.md), [D-58](./D-58.md), [D-64](./D-64.md),
[D-99](./D-99.md), [D-124](./D-124.md) only in the sense of finally agreeing
with them.

**What changed.** Two governing documents disagreed about which market this
product is for, and both were checked in. This decision settles which one is
canonical and leaves the name alone.

1. **`CLAUDE.md`'s opening is canonical** — language teachers and their
   students **anywhere in the world**, neither subject nor country fixed.
2. **The reset's justification for keeping the name has its premise withdrawn
   and its conclusion kept on other grounds.** That document reads _"'AgendaProfe'
   is a Spanish name for a Spanish-speaking market and that is fine. Do not
   rename."_ The verdict stands. The reason given for it does not.

⚠️ **The correction is recorded here rather than there because those reviews are
verbatim and are never edited after the fact.** Editing that document to fix this
would have broken a rule that exists for better reasons than this correction
needs.

**Why the premise is withdrawn.** There is no Spanish-speaking market left in
the code — only MX-shaped defaults and sentinels, each documented as such where
it is declared. A teacher's subject is a BCP-47 code drawn from 234 languages
([D-72](./D-72.md)); she prices in any of ~40 curated currencies
([D-64](./D-64.md)) and banks anywhere through a 15-scheme registry with IBAN
and SWIFT as universal fallbacks ([D-124](./D-124.md)); the platform entity is a
a UK entity ([D-58](./D-58.md)) billing in GBP ([D-99](./D-99.md)) under UK
GDPR ([D-128](./D-128.md)); `DEFAULT_LOCALE` is `en` and the three shipped UI
locales include French. The 2026-08-25 pass retired the last Spanish-first
default (`STARTER_TEMPLATE_LOCALE`) and gave the public funnel its own
`booking_page_locale`, on the strength of the one live Mexican teacher **selling
in English**. A market premise that her own funnel already contradicts is not
the premise to defend the name on.

**Why the conclusion survives anyway.** Three reasons the reset did not use.

1. **The name has no commercial job to do.** Under the same reset, teacher
   acquisition is not an objective and the product's job is to be credible
   evidence of engineering capability at roughly an hour of attention a month.
   A name that is acquiring nobody cannot be improved by changing it.
2. **Nothing better is available.** ~55 candidates were checked on 2026-08-27
   against RDAP, `whois` and a registrar price API. Every short
   language-rooted word is gone across `.com`, `.co` and `.io`, invented ones
   included (`lingora`, `klasora`, `parlaria`, `linguella`, `verbly`,
   `lingwa`, `docenta` — all registered). ⚠️ **RDAP alone reports false
   positives on `.co` and `.io`**: `lingua.co`, `parla.co`, `aula.co`,
   `verba.co`, `parla.io` and `loqui.io` all come back unregistered there and
   all fail a registrar check as premium or held — `lingua.co` is ACTIVE at
   Dynadot. The best genuinely purchasable candidate is **`teachlingua.com`**
   ($11.25/yr, free on 2026-08-27, not reserved by that check). It beats
   `AgendaProfe` on every criterion `AgendaProfe` fails, and not by enough to
   buy a rename with.
3. **The rename is not small.** 2,152 occurrences of the string across 884
   files, plus `agendaprofe.com`, the `agendaprofe` and `agendaprofe-preview`
   Fly apps, `com.agendaprofe.app`, the `agendaprofe` deep-link scheme, both
   pnpm workspace names, `common.brandName` and ~45 further inline occurrences
   in each of three i18n catalogs, and the `utm_content` share-group slugs
   already baked into posted links ([D-125](./D-125.md)).

**What the name actually costs, written down so it is not rediscovered as
news.** _Profe_ is a Latin-American colloquialism for a **schoolteacher** —
opaque to a non-Spanish speaker, and to a Spanish speaker it points at a
classroom rather than at an independent teacher selling private lessons, which
is the wrong segment as well as the wrong language. _Agenda_ is a false friend:
diary in Spanish, meeting-list in English. So to the English-default audience
the code now serves, the compound names the calendar — the least differentiated
part of a product whose edge is the AI lesson layer and the two payment rails.
That cost is real. It is currently paid by nobody, which is the entire argument
above and also its expiry condition.

**What was affected.** This file and its row in [README.md](./README.md). No
code, no copy, no configuration.

**Unresolved risks.**

- **The switching cost is at its lifetime minimum today and only rises.** Brand
  equity is approximately zero at this stage, and `com.agendaprofe.app` is
  uniquely cheap to abandon today — a published listing would make the package
  id permanent. Deferring is a real bet, not a free one.
- **The trigger to reopen this: the first paying teacher who does not read
  Spanish.** At that point the name is being read by somebody it was never for,
  and the decision gets re-costed against whatever is free then. Written down so
  it is not re-litigated by mood.
- **Availability is a snapshot, not a reservation.** `teachlingua.com` was free
  on the day this was written and nothing holds it.
- **The reset's own document still says the old reason** and always will,
  because it cannot be edited. Anyone reading section 6.6 without reaching this
  file gets a market premise the code has not matched since D-72.
