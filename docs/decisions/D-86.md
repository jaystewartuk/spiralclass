# D-86. Financial Intelligence dashboard: a forward-looking cost-estimate layer alongside the actuals, priced provider-agnostically and reported in GBP

**What changed.** A new Super-Admin surface, `/admin/economics`, is added as a
forward-looking **unit-economics estimate layer** — distinct from, and alongside,
the existing backward-looking **actuals** (`/admin/money` revenue-of-record and
`/admin/costs` hand-entered `PlatformExpense`). It models what the platform
_should_ cost from configurable per-integration pricing + manually-entered
monthly usage, to answer "are memberships profitable / what does a teacher or a
lesson cost / when do we exhaust a provider free tier". Full spec:
`docs/product/FINANCIAL_INTELLIGENCE.md`.

Three load-bearing decisions:

- **Pricing is data, not code.** A generic pricing engine
  (`packages/shared/src/economics-pricing.ts`) evaluates a Zod discriminated
  union (`free` / `monthly` / `annual` / `payg` / `tiered` / `hybrid`, with
  free-tier allowances folded in) against usage meters. There are **no
  per-provider branches** — a provider's specifics live in an `Integration` row's
  `pricingModel` JSON. Adding an integration needs no migration and no code, the
  same migration-free posture `PlatformExpense.vendor` already takes (TEXT
  validated vs a shared const, not a DB enum). Categories follow suit: a new
  13-value `INTEGRATION_CATEGORIES` shared const, **not** an extension of the
  7-value `ExpenseCategory` enum (which is load-bearing for the actuals page).

- **GBP is the money-of-record for this view.** Per D-58 the platform entity is a
  UK-established, so the operator reasons about profitability in pounds. The engine
  is currency-pure (returns native minor units); a single admin-editable FX
  assumption (`EconomicsAssumptions` singleton: `fxUsdToGbp` / `fxMxnToGbp` /
  `fxEurToGbp`) converts both provider costs (mostly USD) **and** the reused
  MXN-centavos revenue series to one blended GBP total. This mirrors
  `money-metrics.ts`'s refusal to silently blend currencies — FX is an explicit,
  visible, dated assumption, not a hidden constant.

- **Estimates never touch actuals.** The estimate layer reads the same Postgres
  revenue via `getSubscriptionOverview` / `getCommissionSeries` but writes
  nothing back and is never folded into `/admin/money`'s P&L. Estimate-vs-actual
  reconciliation is a deferred future phase, not part of the money-of-record.

**Why it likely changed.** Pricing/tier decisions were being made without a model
of unit economics. The actuals surfaces answer "what were we billed" but not
"what _should_ one teacher/lesson cost" or "when do we blow past a free tier" —
questions that need usage-driven estimation, not invoiced lump sums. Building it
as a separate estimate layer (rather than extending `/admin/costs`) keeps the
authoritative P&L uncontaminated by modelling assumptions, and the
provider-agnostic engine is the one abstraction expensive to get wrong, so it was
locked first (schema + engine before any CRUD/UI).

**What was affected.** New: `packages/shared/src/economics-config.ts` +
`economics-pricing.ts` (+ tests); `apps/web` Prisma models `Integration`,
`UsageInput`, `EconomicsAssumptions`; `apps/web/src/lib/economics/*`;
`apps/web/src/app/actions/admin-economics.ts`; `apps/web/src/app/admin/economics/*`;
a shared `StatCard` extracted to `apps/web/src/components/ui/stat.tsx` (replacing
the triplicated local `Stat`); one `NAV` entry (`section: money`,
`minRole: finance`) in `apps/web/src/app/admin/layout.tsx`; `economics.*` i18n
keys in all three locales. Reuses `money-metrics.ts`, `subscriptions/admin-metrics.ts`,
`writeOverride` (system target), the `CategoryBarChart`/`DataTable` primitives,
and the `admin-costs.ts` action pattern.

**Unresolved risks.**

- **FX drift.** Rates are hand-entered and can go stale; the dashboard shows
  "FX as of {date}" and surfaces unknown-currency integrations separately rather
  than guessing. A real FX feed is out of scope.
- **JSON pricing model is unvalidated at the DB.** A malformed `pricingModel`
  can't be caught by Postgres. Mitigated by Zod-parsing at the read boundary, a
  `schemaVersion` field, and fail-soft rendering (an unparseable model
  contributes £0 + a visible warning badge, never a 500).
- **Allocation-basis arbitrariness.** Membership margins depend on how shared
  infra cost is split across tiers; the basis is configurable
  (`allocationBasis`) and labelled directional, not GAAP.
- **Two cost surfaces, two currencies** (estimates GBP, actuals MXN) risk
  operator confusion; mitigated by a distinct label and an "Estimated — not the
  money of record" banner.
