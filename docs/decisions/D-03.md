# D-03. Recurring monthly subscriptions removed

**What changed.** An earlier iteration of the product offered monthly subscription packages (where the student paid every month and received a fixed quota of classes). This has been removed in favor of fixed one-off package purchases ("buy 8 classes, use them within N months, then buy again").

**Why it likely changed.**

- Subscriptions added significant complexity: pre-approvals, renewal failures, grace periods, monthly slot quotas, automatic re-materialization of bookings each month.
- The team appears to have decided the simpler one-off package model is enough for the alpha and reduces support load.

**What was affected.**

- Removed: monthly preapproval handling, renewal-failure grace period, monthly slot quota enforcement, recurring booking materializer.
- Simplified: payment state machine, package lifecycle, the public purchase page.

**Unresolved risks.**

- A handful of background events related to the removed subscription path still existed in event-type definitions until this cleanup pass; they have now been removed.
- Any teacher who was promised a monthly subscription product before the pivot will need a migration story.
