# D-02. Cash-voucher (OXXO) payment rail removed

**What changed.** OXXO (Mexico's cash-voucher rail, where the buyer takes a barcode to a convenience store and pays in cash) used to be a supported payment method. It has been removed: students can now only pay by card.

**Why it likely changed.**

- OXXO settles asynchronously (the buyer has up to several days to actually pay), which complicated booking — the student couldn't be confirmed into the calendar until the cash landed.
- Card-only is simpler operationally and aligned with international students.

**What was affected.**

- Public booking purchase page copy.
- Stripe checkout configuration.
- Payment-method tracking field (the "rail" was reduced from {card, oxxo, unknown} to {card, unknown}).
- Privacy notice copy.

**Unresolved risks.**

- Mexico-only students who do not have a card may now be excluded. Whether this segment is meaningful for the alpha is a product question, not a code question.
