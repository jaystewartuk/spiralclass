# D-05. Single-team, single-product privacy framing

**Content removed 2026-09-06, before publication ([D-159](./D-159.md)).**

**What this record covered.** Which party the privacy notice names as the data
controller — the platform itself, rather than each teacher for her own students —
and the reasoning about legal responsibility that followed from it.

**Why it is not published.** It is a legal position rather than an engineering
decision. What the product actually states is published in full and is the thing
to read instead: [D-128](./D-128.md), and the policy itself as structured data in
`packages/shared/src/legal/privacy-policy.ts`, with the supplier register
alongside it in `legal/subprocessors.ts` and a guard test holding the two against
the vendor credentials `env.ts` declares.

⚠️ **It is superseded, not merely removed.** [D-128](./D-128.md) rewrote the
privacy layer wholesale; the framing this record described predates it.
