# D-192. An operator's test accounts are marked, and left out of every figure — not tested inside a real teacher's account unmarked (2026-10-02)

**Status.** Active.

**What changed.** `Teacher` and `Student` have a `testAccount` flag. An admin
sets it in the console (teacher or student page), with a required reason,
audited through `writeOverride`. It is read-side only, and it changes which
figures an account counts in:

- **A real teacher's screens** leave her test students out of:
  - earned by month and "this month";
  - paid in advance;
  - booked for the rest of the month;
  - the roster's figures and "needs attention".

  Their rows stay visible, marked "Cuenta de prueba", in the roster and in the
  payments ledger. A test charge is real money on her own Stripe account, and
  the ledger has to reconcile to it.

- **A test teacher's screens** show everything, test students included. That
  account is the operator's sandbox, and its point is to show what a teacher
  would see.
- **Platform roll-ups** leave out test teachers and test students:
  - admin overview totals;
  - signups;
  - GMV;
  - subscription revenue and overview;
  - platform paid-in-advance.
- **The sitemap** leaves out test teachers.
- **PostHog** gets an `is_test_account` person property from both the server
  and the client identify, so its "filter out test accounts" switch can be
  keyed on it.

`lib/marketing/test-accounts.ts` is the one definition. A row is a test account
if it is **recorded** (this flag) or matches the **shape** rule (seed and fixture
slugs, `.test` and `@example.com` emails). Every exclusion reads its `where`
fragments from that module.

**Why.** The operator tested live flows as a student inside a real teacher's
account. On 2 October her money screen said "Pagado por adelantado $6,900 · 126
clases por impartir". 100 of those classes were a test account's $0 package.
Her ledger also listed four failed payments by the operator under his own name.
Nothing marked any of it, so she could not tell it apart from her business, and
neither could the platform's own totals.

**What was considered and rejected.**

- **Infer test accounts from email or slug.** Already done for fixtures, and
  kept for them. But the operator's own test student uses a real address, and
  the `Student.seededAt` note records how email inference broke silently when
  the product was renamed (D-138). A recorded flag cannot rot that way.
- **Hide a test student from the teacher entirely.** Her ledger would then
  stop reconciling to her Stripe account, and a test charge she can see in
  Stripe would be missing here. Marked rather than hidden.
- **Delete the test data.** "Never delete teacher data" is a standing rule,
  and the rows are real history: real failed charges, real bookings.
- **Only ever test in a separate test teacher.** That is the recommended
  practice, and the flag supports it: a test teacher is a sandbox that still
  shows everything. But testing a real teacher's live funnel as a buyer is
  sometimes the only way to check it, so the student-side flag is needed too.
- **Reuse `TeacherSubscription.comped`.** That is billing ("full Pro, never
  billed"), a separate axis. A test teacher's billing stays an explicit comped
  action, not a side effect of this flag.

**What it constrains.**

- The flag is set only by the admin action. It is never set by self-serve, a
  teacher, a student or an API handler, and never inferred.
- It never gates sign-in, booking, payment or entitlements, and never hides or
  deletes rows. It is an exclusion from figures only.
- A new figure over teachers, students, packages, bookings or payments must
  take its exclusion from `lib/marketing/test-accounts.ts`:
  - `realStudentsOf(teacher)` for one teacher's figures;
  - `REAL_PACKAGE_WHERE` / `NOT_TEST_ACCOUNT` for platform roll-ups.

  Restating it locally is how a rule drifts (D-142).

- Practice: test inside a test teacher. When a real teacher's live page must be
  tested as a buyer, mark that student first, and refund the charge after.

**What was affected.**

- Schema: `teachers.test_account`, `students.test_account` (migration
  `20261002010000_test_accounts`, additive and defaulted, per D-178).
- `lib/marketing/test-accounts.ts`.
- `lib/cashflow.ts`, `lib/money-metrics.ts`, `lib/admin-metrics.ts`,
  `lib/subscriptions/admin-metrics.ts`.
- `app/admin/page.tsx`, `app/admin/{teachers,students}/[id]/page.tsx`,
  `app/admin/test-account-form.tsx`, `app/actions/admin-test-accounts.ts`.
- `app/(app)/dashboard/students/page.tsx`, the roster list, and the payments
  ledger.
- `lib/analytics/posthog.ts`, `components/posthog-identify.tsx`, and the
  identify calls in `lib/auth.ts` and `lib/api/auth.ts`.

**Trade-offs accepted, and unresolved risks.**

- **Not everywhere yet.** Admin CSV exports, the ledger's month "received"
  subtotals, the commission report and Wise renewals still count test rows.
  These are operator surfaces, where the rows are visible anyway, or cash that
  genuinely landed.
- **Nothing enforces the rule mechanically.** No test enumerates every figure
  and checks it uses the module, so a new figure can still forget it. A guard
  that greps the roll-up modules for the import would close that.
- **PostHog history is not rewritten.** The property applies from the next
  identify or layout mount; earlier events stay attributed as they were.
- **Ana's account still has to be marked.** Her account becomes correct only
  once an admin marks the operator's test students, after this ships.
