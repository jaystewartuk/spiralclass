# D-132. A teacher setting that auto-starts class recording

**What changed.** A new per-teacher opt-in, `Teacher.autoRecordClasses`
(default false), toggled in Settings → Account. When it is on, the server
starts the class's room-composite recording itself once **both** the teacher and
the student are in the room, instead of waiting for the teacher to tap Record.
the operator's instruction, 2026-08-26, directly after [D-131](./D-131.md).

**Why.** D-131 turned recording and transcription back on and, in doing so,
made a pre-existing weakness load-bearing: the entire lesson-insights pipeline —
recording, per-speaker audio, transcript, focus areas, the pre-class brief —
began at one easily-forgotten button press, mid-lesson, by someone who is busy
teaching. A feature whose value depends on a human remembering a discretionary
tap during their actual job is a feature that mostly does not happen. This moves
the trigger to the one moment that is unambiguous and observable: the class has
begun, because both people are in it.

**What it deliberately does NOT change.** This decides **who starts** a
recording, never **what a recording may capture**. Everything protective already
lived inside `startBookingRecording`, and the new entry point calls it rather
than reimplementing any of it:

- **The D-22 per-student insights consent still decides whose voice is
  analysed**, guardian-only for a minor, and is entirely separate from this
  toggle. A teacher opting into auto-record grants herself nothing about her
  students. This separation is the point: a teacher's preference about her own
  classes is not a consent decision on someone else's behalf, and the two must
  not be collapsed into one switch.
- **Both parties still see the recording indicator.** The web call UI mirrors
  LiveKit's own `room.isRecording` to teacher and student alike
  (`components/video/class-call.tsx`), so an auto-started recording is exactly
  as visible as a hand-started one. There is no silent recording, and this
  change did not need to add anything to keep that true.
- **The Pro gate and `recordingEnabled()` still apply**, unchanged, because they
  sit inside the reused function.

**How the trigger works** (`maybeAutoStartRecording`,
`lib/video/call-recording.ts`, called from the `participant_joined` webhook):

1. `recordingEnabled()` — the environment-level gate, checked first and
   cheapest.
2. The teacher's `autoRecordClasses` must be on.
3. **The booking must have no `CallRecording` row at all** — not merely no
   _active_ one. This is what makes an explicit Stop stick. Reconnects re-fire
   `participant_joined` routinely, so a guard of "nothing is currently
   recording" would silently restart a recording the teacher had just chosen to
   end. Auto-start gets one turn per booking; after that the control is hers.
4. **Both identities must be present in the room.** A teacher who opens the call
   early is not in a class; recording her waiting room captures her alone and
   spends egress CPU on a 2-OCPU box for nothing.

Best-effort throughout: it is called from a webhook, so nothing it does may
surface as a webhook error.

**What was affected.** `prisma/schema.prisma` plus the migration
`20260826000000_add_teacher_auto_record_classes` (NOT NULL DEFAULT false, so
every existing teacher keeps today's manual-only behaviour on deploy);
`lib/video/call-recording.ts` (the new function); `lib/video/webhook-events.ts`
(one call on `participant_joined`); `app/actions/profile.ts`
(`saveAutoRecordClassesAction`); `settings/account/auto-record-classes-form.tsx`
and its card in `settings/account/page.tsx`; six i18n keys across all three
catalogs; `tests/video/auto-record.test.ts` (10 tests) and two additions to
`tests/video/webhook-events.test.ts`.

The settings card is hidden unless `recordingEnabled()` — the same rule the
in-call Record control follows, so the toggle never appears somewhere it could
not do anything.

**Web only.** This was not asked for anywhere else, and needs nothing there:
the trigger is a server-side webhook, so a class is auto-recorded whatever
surface it was taken on.

**Unresolved risks.**

- **It does not fix the track-publication race** that D-131 named. Auto-start
  reaches `startLessonAudioCaptures` at the moment the second participant
  joins, which is exactly when that participant's microphone track may not be
  published yet. So auto-record makes the A/V recording reliable — that is
  room-composite and does not depend on individual tracks — while the
  per-speaker insights capture keeps the same best-effort character it had
  before. Fixing it properly means handling a `track_published` webhook, which
  is a new normalized event kind and its own change.
- **Every auto-recorded class costs money that a forgotten tap did not.** The
  Deepgram bill is per audio-minute and capture is per speaker, so switching
  this on for a teacher roughly means her full timetable is transcribed at two
  audio-minutes per class-minute. That is the intended effect, not a surprise,
  but it is the first setting in the product whose default-off state was also
  saving the operator money.
- **A recording started by the server is a recording nobody consciously
  began.** The indicator makes it visible and Stop remains available, but the
  psychology differs from a deliberate tap, and a student joining a class that
  is already recording has less of a moment to object than one who watches the
  teacher press a button. Judged acceptable because the indicator is
  unmissable, the teacher opted in knowingly, and voice analysis — the part
  that produces lasting data about the student — remains behind that student's
  own recorded consent.
- **No retention or purge work is included.** D-131's open gap stands: nothing
  removes recordings from R2 when an account closes, and auto-record produces
  strictly more of them.
