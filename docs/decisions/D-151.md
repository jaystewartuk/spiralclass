# D-151. A "Verified student" testimonial is written by the student, never by the teacher, and the database refuses a badge without one

A testimonial with `source = 'student_submitted'` is written by a signed-in
student with at least one completed class with that teacher, from
`/my-classes/teachers/<id>`. It publishes on arrival, and the public booking
page shows it as **"Verified student · N classes with her"**, where N is
counted from completed bookings at render time. The teacher can hide or delete
one and cannot write or edit one. A CHECK constraint makes `student_id` and
`verified_at` required for that source and forbidden for `teacher_curated`.

## Why

Testimonials do not stay on one page. The acquisition planner raises
`no_testimonials` and schedules a `testimonial` content kind that republishes
quotes into Facebook groups and Reddit
([student acquisition](../features/student-acquisition.md),
[D-125](./D-125.md)). It can check that a quote exists, but not that it is
true. So the failure was never one exaggerated page: the platform was
amplifying an unverifiable claim into public communities on its own schedule.

The platform entity is UK-established ([D-143](./D-143.md)). The DMCC Act 2024's
fake-review provisions ban publishing fake consumer reviews **and facilitating
them**. That is a factor, not legal advice, and no solicitor wrote this record.

## Options considered

- **No student reviews until there is volume and moderation** — the schema's
  original stance. That argument holds against **ratings**. Against
  testimonials it inverts: one quote the platform can vouch for beats one it
  cannot at any n. A curated quote asks the reader to trust the teacher; a
  student's quote can only be hidden by her.
- **Hold a verified quote for teacher approval** — a page showing only the
  verified quotes its owner liked is curated again. Hiding one is a visible act
  on a row she can see.
- **Store the class count on the row** — it drifts from the next lesson on,
  and "12 classes" against a database that says 40 is a lie in the one place
  the feature asks to be believed.
- **Enforce only in application code** — one missed filter, such as a bulk
  import or a route that forgets a column, could mint a badge. The constraint
  makes `verified_at` unwritable without the student the claim is about.

## What it cost

- **Discovery.** A student finds the form only by visiting the teacher's page
  in the portal. Asking students by email is an outbound message to her
  customers on her behalf. It needs a per-teacher opt-in, a rate limit and the
  notification opt-out paths, and is not built. The intended shape is the HMAC
  link in `lib/notifications/settings-link-token.ts` through `enqueue`. Uptake
  is likely low, so many pages will show no badge for a while.
- **Existing quotes were not marked "unverified".** That would have punished
  teachers for the platform's earlier design. So the contrast only works on a
  page with at least one verified quote.
- **Verification is not endorsement.** A teacher can ask a happy student to
  write one and coach it. The badge claims only that a real, taught student
  wrote it.

## What it constrains

- **Every teacher-side write filters on `source: "teacher_curated"`**
  ([`lib/testimonials/store.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/testimonials/store.ts)),
  so an edit aimed at a verified row returns not-found
  (`tests/actions/testimonials-action.test.ts`, "scopes by teacherId and
  source"). Do not add a teacher write path without that filter.
- **The CHECK constraint `testimonials_verified_shape` and the partial unique
  index on `(teacher_id, student_id)`** (one testimonial per student per
  teacher) live in the hand-written invariants migration. The drift check
  compares models and cannot see them. Neither has its own integration test;
  they are exercised only when the migration runs against a real database.
- **Standing has one source.** The write path and the public page both read it
  from [`lib/testimonials/eligibility.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/testimonials/eligibility.ts)
  (`tests/testimonials/eligibility.test.ts`). `no_show` does not count: the
  class was paid for, but nobody was taught.
- **The student's id and name come from the roster, never from the form.** A
  refusal reads the same whether there is no pairing or no completed class, so
  a stranger cannot probe pairings
  (`tests/actions/student-testimonials-action.test.ts`).
- **Never emit `Review` or `AggregateRating` JSON-LD.** Unrated quotes would
  break Google's review-snippet policy, and a verified quote still carries no
  rating. [`tests/seo/jsonld.test.ts`](../../apps/web/tests/seo/jsonld.test.ts)
  ("carries no offers or ratings") pins it.
- **The student FK cascades on delete.** Once the student is erased the claim
  cannot be proved, and an orphan that still says "verified student" is the
  exact failure this exists to prevent.

## How we got here

- **Before September 2026.** `testimonials` held teacher-curated quotes only,
  and its schema comment deferred student reviews for "volume + moderation".
  `content-kinds.ts` already noted that a testimonial post with no published
  testimonial "produces a fabrication".
- **Early September 2026 (undated).** Added `source`, `student_id` and `verified_at`. The
  migration was additive, and every existing row became `teacher_curated`,
  rendering exactly as before. The teacher's editor shows verified rows
  read-only, in the same list as her own, because the comparison shows her
  what the page claims. A student can withdraw their own quote, including
  after they stop being eligible.
- **2026-09-06.** The schema was squashed. The constraint and the partial index
  now live in the invariants migration
  (`20260906120100_database_invariants`).
