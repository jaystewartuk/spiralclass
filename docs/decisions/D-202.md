# D-202. An AI materials call records what it returned and whether the teacher kept it, and the text is cleared after 30 days

**Status.** Active.

**What changed.** The `class_content_generations` row that every successful
materials call already wrote for the monthly cap now also records the kind of
call, the text the teacher received, the refine instruction behind it, and
`keptAt` with the material id once a save of AI-sourced content contained that
text. The text and instruction are cleared after 30 days by the existing daily
materials purge, and on account deletion. The rows stay, because the cap
counts them.

**Why.** Measured on 2026-10-16. PostHog shows the one real teacher ran about
56 generations and 55 refines between July and mid-October. Production holds
13 materials with an AI revision from her, 17 AI revisions in all, and **zero**
manual edits after any of them. She never fixes the model's text by hand: she
asks for another version, and when it is still wrong she walks away. Both of
those verdicts were invisible. The quota row stored a timestamp, the refine
event stored the instruction's length, and a discarded draft left nothing.

Without those verdicts there is nothing to build a materials eval set from,
and materials is the AI surface she uses most.

**What was considered and rejected.**

- _Hand-edit diffs from `MaterialRevision`._ The first idea, and the
  measurement above is what killed it: there are none. Revisions also
  snapshot the body being overwritten rather than the one saved, and are
  capped at 20 per material, so they cannot carry a verdict anyway.
- _A generation id passed through the editor and back on save._ Exact, but it
  threads an id through three client components and the per-section refine
  splice. The server-side containment match is good enough: a save whose
  source is `ai` and whose body contains a recent generation's output kept
  that output. A teacher who types into the text flips the source to
  `manual`, so her own words are never credited to the model.
- _The instruction text in PostHog._ It is the teacher's own words about her
  lesson, and analytics is the wrong place for content (D-23).
- _A new table._ The quota row already exists one-to-one with each call, and a
  second table would have to be kept in step with it.
- _A new cron._ Every distinct cron minute is its own database wake. The
  purge is a second step of `materials-purge-cron`, on both job backends.

**What it constrains.**

- Every materials model call writes through `recordMaterialGeneration` in
  `apps/web/src/lib/materials/generations.ts`, never a bare
  `classContentGeneration.create`.
- `keptAt` is set only from a save whose source is `ai`. Never infer it from a
  manual save.
- Generated text is not kept past `GENERATION_BODY_RETENTION_DAYS`. Raising it
  is a decision, not a constant change.
- The instruction and body never go to PostHog or Sentry.

**What was affected.** `prisma/schema.prisma` and migration
`20261016120000_material_generation_verdicts` (additive, nullable). The
materials handlers, the generate stream route, the podcast quota write, the
materials purge on both job backends, teacher anonymisation, and
`docs/features/library-materials.md`.

**Trade-offs accepted, and unresolved risks.**

- Containment can over-credit. If two drafts in a day share a section and she
  saves one, both are marked kept. The figure is a ceiling on usefulness.
- A refine chain marks only the last link kept. The earlier links each have
  the next instruction as their verdict, which is the point.
- A generation saved more than 24 hours later is not matched.
- The privacy policy says the text of a material is sent to Anthropic. It does
  not say a draft she discarded is kept for 30 days. That sentence may need
  adding, and legal text needs a person's translation (D-196). Unresolved.
- Rows written before this change have no kind and no text, so the first
  usable month of data starts on the day this deploys.
