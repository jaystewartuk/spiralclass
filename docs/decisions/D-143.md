# D-143. The teacher is the merchant of record: lesson payments are direct charges on her own Stripe account, and the platform takes nothing at the charge

Every lesson payment on the card rail is a Stripe Checkout Session created on
the teacher's connected account with the `Stripe-Account` header, with no
`application_fee_amount` and no `transfer_data`, so the money settles on her
balance in her country and the platform never holds, withholds or forwards any
of it. Her account is an Accounts v2 `Account` with `dashboard: "full"`, the
`merchant` and `customer` configurations, and `fees_collector` and
`losses_collector` both `"stripe"`: Stripe bills her its processing fee at her
country's rate and debits her balance for a chargeback. The platform's own
Stripe entity is UK-established and charges her only for her SpiralClass
subscription, billed to that same `Account` through `customer_account`.

## Why

Collecting a student's money and passing it on to a teacher is the activity
that needs payment-services authorisation. The regulatory analysis is not
published here ([D-158](./D-158.md)). The engineering rule that follows from it
is that **the platform must not be the party that receives a student's
money**. Connect is the way Stripe's licences cover the teacher's leg.

Inside that rule, the funds flow decides three things. The first is which
countries can be served. A Transfer from a UK platform only reaches the
cross-border payout circle, which is GB, US, CA, CH and the EEA. The second is
which payment methods a buyer is offered, because Checkout offers the
**merchant's** country's local methods. The third is who a dispute debits.

## Options considered

- **Mercado Pago as the platform's processor** (the first version) — lost to
  Stripe Connect for international cards and self-serve onboarding.
- **A local platform entity per market** — each entity covers one corridor
  (domestic platform to domestic teachers), so every new market needs a new
  entity.
- **Separate charges and transfers from a UK entity** (July to August 2026) —
  card payments were limited to the circle, and the launch market, Brazil,
  Japan, Singapore, Thailand and Australia were all outside it. Checkout could
  offer only methods a UK merchant can offer, so no OXXO, SPEI, Boleto, iDEAL
  or BLIK. Disputes debited the platform first, to be clawed back from a
  teacher who may already have withdrawn.
- **The platform collects and redistributes** (its own Stripe or Wise balance,
  Stripe Global Payouts, Mercado Pago split payments, dLocal, EBANX) — each
  makes the platform the collector or hands it the compliance Connect absorbs.
- **Teacher-held third-party card links** as manual-rail instruments (a Wise
  payment link, a Mercado Pago link de pago, PayPal) — chosen on 2026-08-28 and
  never built. A direct charge makes her the merchant of record on Stripe
  itself, with no second processor, dispute model or per-teacher link to
  check. Such a link stays admissible only as a validated instrument kind
  ([D-145](./D-145.md)).
- **Accounts v1** (Standard accounts with OAuth) — would have kept Pix and
  allowed connecting an account she already has. Lost because v2's `customer`
  configuration removes the `Account`-to-`Customer` mapping table for billing.

## What it cost

- **No take-rate at the charge.** The 8, 3 or 0 percent commission withheld
  from the settled net was removed. Collecting one now would need an
  application fee, and cross-border application-fee support is undocumented
  and known to fail in the markets being opened.
- **Countries refused.** IN refuses `card_payments`. ZA, NG, ID and IS refuse
  `configuration.merchant`. Iceland was inside the old circle, so it is a real
  narrowing. These teachers have Wise only.
- **Pix is unreachable** on v2. The Dashboard labels it "Only supported in v1
  accounts", along with PayPal, Alipay, WeChat Pay, Bizum, UPI and PayTo.
- **An existing Stripe account can never be connected** (v2 has no OAuth), so
  she ends up with two dashboards and knows the wrong one.
- **Every account the platform creates is permanent.** Stripe refuses
  `DELETE /v1/accounts/{id}` and the v2 close call with
  `stripe_loss_liable_cannot_be_deleted`.
- **The API pin jumped** from `2024-06-20` to `2026-06-24.dahlia`.
- **A saved card belongs to one teacher.** Under direct charges the charge is
  already on her account, so Strong Customer Authentication follows her
  country and `on_behalf_of` is not needed; but the Customer and card live on
  her account, so a student who buys from two teachers saves a card twice.
  (An earlier version of this record listed SCA as unsolved; the later
  regulatory review found it is not.)
- **She cannot see her real fee in SpiralClass**, only a pointer to her
  Stripe dashboard.

## What it constrains

- **Take nothing at the charge.** Never set `application_fee_amount` or
  `transfer_data`, and never bring back a Transfer, a payout read-back or a
  reversal on refund. Guarded by "takes no cut: neither an application fee nor a
  transfer" in
  [direct-charge-checkout.test.ts](../../apps/web/tests/stripe/direct-charge-checkout.test.ts).
  A take-rate, if ever wanted, is a **billing** feature metered from the
  webhook (comment in
  [subscriptions-config.ts](../../packages/shared/src/subscriptions-config.ts)).
- **Subscription billing is a platform charge.** It uses the platform key,
  no `Stripe-Account` header, its own webhook endpoint and secret, and
  `customer_account` on her own `Account`, with no platform `Customer`. A
  teacher Stripe refuses as a merchant still gets a `customer`-only Account.
- **The country list is measured, not inferred.**
  `SUPPORTED_CONNECT_COUNTRIES` in
  [countries.ts](../../packages/shared/src/countries.ts) is pinned by
  "lists exactly the measured merchant-capable set" and "rejects IS" in
  `countries.test.ts`. Run
  [probe-v2-capabilities.mjs](../../scripts/probe-v2-capabilities.mjs) before
  adding a country or a capability: v2 400s on an unknown capability name.
  `V2_REJECTED_CAPABILITIES` in
  [stripe-capabilities.ts](../../packages/shared/src/stripe-capabilities.ts)
  records the two that did this, with a test.
- **A new local payment method needs two things:** the capability requested at
  account creation, and the method switched on in the platform's
  connected-account payment method configuration in the Stripe Dashboard
  (Settings → Connect → Payment methods). The API cannot change that parent
  configuration. With the capability active and the parent switched off,
  checkout silently offers cards only. Do not pin `payment_method_types`.
- **Treat account creation as irreversible.** The idempotency key
  `connect-account-${teacher.id}` must never become optional or random. The
  guarded persist in
  [stripe-connect.ts](../../apps/web/src/app/actions/stripe-connect.ts)
  (`updateMany` on `stripeAccountId: null`, adopting the winner's id if the
  claim loses) belongs with it. Guarded in
  [connected-account-v2.test.ts](../../apps/web/tests/stripe/connected-account-v2.test.ts).
- **No "paste your Stripe account id" field.** The platform cannot reach an
  account it did not create, so the first charge would fail with
  `account_invalid`.
- **Pin the SDK's bundled stable API version** (`STRIPE_API_VERSION` in
  [client.ts](../../apps/web/src/lib/stripe/client.ts)), never a `.preview`
  one without a probe that needs it.
- **The fee is Stripe's, and copy says so.** Teacher-facing copy names who
  charges the fee, says SpiralClass receives none of it, and links to
  `STRIPE_PRICING_URL`; public marketing quotes no percentage.
  `STRIPE_WORST_CASE_RATE` in
  [pricing-fees.ts](../../packages/shared/src/pricing-fees.ts) is our own
  generous estimate for sizing the Wise discount, never Stripe's rate. SPEI
  inside Stripe checkout carries Stripe's fee, so copy names **Wise**, not
  "bank transfer", as the rail that skips Stripe. Guarded by "the card fee is
  disclosed, and attributed to Stripe" in
  [i18n.test.ts](../../packages/shared/src/i18n/i18n.test.ts).
- **The student checkout asks only for the address the payment method
  needs.** `billing_address_collection` on the lesson checkout is `required`
  only when `STRIPE_TAX_ENABLED` is on; the subscription checkout always
  requires it. The flag has prerequisites outside the code it does not
  enforce. See [tax.ts](../../apps/web/src/lib/stripe/tax.ts). Guarded by
  "startCheckout — billing address" in
  [start-checkout.test.ts](../../apps/web/tests/payments/start-checkout.test.ts)
  and by
  [checkout-billing-capture.test.ts](../../apps/web/tests/stripe/checkout-billing-capture.test.ts).
- **Reversing v2 means giving up the `customer` configuration**, not just
  changing onboarding.

## How we got here

- **Before launch:** Mercado Pago was replaced by Stripe Connect, with each
  teacher paid into her own Stripe account.
- **July 2026:** the platform's Stripe entity moved to the UK, the gate became
  the cross-border circle (dropping the launch market), and lessons ran as
  separate charges and transfers less a commission. Both checkouts began
  requiring a full billing address so tax could later be switched on without a
  backfill.
- **2026-08-28:** "the platform never touches the funds" became a constraint.
  Teacher-held card links were chosen and never built.
- **2026-08-30:** direct charges on Accounts v2 replaced that plan. Every
  country code was probed in test mode with the exact configuration the app
  creates: 44 accepted, five refused. The commission, `payments/transfer.ts`,
  its Inngest job, the refund clawback and three `payments` columns were
  deleted, and subscription billing was rebuilt on `customer_account`.
  A probe showed the stable `2026-06-24.dahlia`, not a preview version,
  creates the same account. `booking_page_viewed`
  had carried `stripeReady: false` on every non-owner view from 2026-07-29
  until about 20:40 that day, so no visitor had ever been offered a card. The
  buy page was rebuilt around the buyer's order (see
  [payments](../features/payments.md)), and the student checkout stopped
  requiring the full address.
- **2026-08-31:** a live checkout on an early teacher's account offered cards
  only although `oxxo_payments` and `mx_bank_transfer_payments` were active,
  because her payment method configuration inherited them as off from the UK
  parent. After the Dashboard switch a session showed
  `["card", "oxxo", "customer_balance", "link"]`. `pix_payments` (not a v2
  field) had been failing every Brazilian account creation with a 400, and
  `fpx_payments` failed without `business_type`; both were removed. A CSP
  header missing `connect-js.stripe.com` made the onboarding card retry with a
  fresh random idempotency key, creating **24 permanent orphan live accounts**
  for one teacher in about ninety minutes. No card had yet been charged.
- **Days later:** the fee disclosure shipped. "Getting paid is always free"
  had appeared in five places, two found only by grep. The template editor
  had called our 5.25% bound "what Stripe charges", against 1.5% for UK
  domestic cards. Reverting the English catalog fails six of the guards.
